2ADVANTAGES & DISADVANTAGES ESSAY / সুবিধা ও অসুবিধার রচনা

This type requires balanced coverage. If asked 'What are the advantages and disadvantages?', write one paragraph on each side with roughly equal depth. If asked 'Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?', take a clear position in your introduction and conclusion on which side is stronger — but still discuss both sides in the body.

এই ধরনে সুষম কভারেজ প্রয়োজন। 'What are the advantages and disadvantages?' জিজ্ঞেস করলে, প্রায় সমান গভীরতায় প্রতিটি দিকে একটি paragraph লিখুন। 'Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?' জিজ্ঞেস করলে, আপনার introduction ও conclusion-এ কোন দিক শক্তিশালী তা স্পষ্টভাবে বলুন — তবে body-তে উভয় দিক নিয়ে আলোচনা করুন।

🎯 Key strategy / মূল কৌশল

Both body paragraphs must be roughly equal in length and development — the examiner is checking balance. A common mistake is writing a long advantages paragraph and a very short disadvantages paragraph (or vice versa).

উভয় body paragraph প্রায় সমান দৈর্ঘ্য ও development-এ হতে হবে — পরীক্ষক ভারসাম্য পরীক্ষা করছেন। একটি সাধারণ ভুল হলো advantages paragraph লম্বা এবং disadvantages paragraph খুব সংক্ষিপ্ত লেখা।

2a. Model question & full answer / মডেল প্রশ্ন ও পূর্ণ উত্তর

Model Question / মডেল প্রশ্ন

Some countries are considering making all secondary school students wear uniforms. What are the advantages and disadvantages of introducing school uniforms?

কিছু দেশ সমস্ত মাধ্যমিক বিদ্যালয়ের শিক্ষার্থীদের ইউনিফর্ম পরার বিষয়ে বিবেচনা করছে। স্কুল ইউনিফর্ম প্রবর্তনের সুবিধা ও অসুবিধা কী?

Full model answer (Band 7–8) / পূর্ণ মডেল উত্তর:

[Introduction] The question of whether secondary school students should wear a standard uniform is one that generates strong opinions among educators, parents, and students alike. Both the potential benefits and the drawbacks of this policy deserve careful consideration.

[Body 1 — Advantages] There are several compelling advantages to introducing school uniforms. Most significantly, uniforms remove visible markers of economic difference, reducing the potential for clothing-related social judgement and bullying. When all students wear the same attire, wealth disparities are less obvious, creating a more equitable social environment within the school. Furthermore, uniforms foster a sense of shared identity and belonging, which can strengthen school community cohesion. In countries such as the United Kingdom, where school uniforms have a long history, many educators report that a consistent dress code helps establish a professional, focused learning atmosphere.

[Body 2 — Disadvantages] On the other hand, school uniforms carry notable disadvantages. Firstly, they can suppress the individual self-expression that is particularly important during adolescence, a period when young people are actively developing their personal identities. Clothing is one of the primary ways teenagers communicate who they are, and removing this freedom may hinder that development. Additionally, uniforms impose a financial burden on families who must purchase specific items that can only be used for school, which may be especially challenging for lower-income households.

[Conclusion] In conclusion, school uniforms offer genuine benefits in terms of social equality and school identity, while also presenting challenges related to individual expression and family finances. On balance, I believe the advantages outweigh the disadvantages when schools provide financial support for lower-income families and allow some degree of personal expression within the uniform policy.

(282 words)

3PROBLEM & SOLUTION ESSAY / সমস্যা ও সমাধানের রচনা

This type asks you to explain why something is a problem and suggest how to fix it. The structure is straightforward: Body 1 covers the causes or problems, Body 2 covers the solutions. Both must have real development — not just lists.

এই ধরনে কিছু কেন সমস্যা তা ব্যাখ্যা করতে হয় এবং কীভাবে সমাধান করা যায় তা পরামর্শ দিতে হয়। কাঠামো সহজ: Body 1 কারণ বা সমস্যা কভার করে, Body 2 সমাধান কভার করে। উভয়েরই প্রকৃত বিকাশ থাকতে হবে — শুধু তালিকা নয়।

🎯 Language tip — Solutions need justification / Solutions-এর যুক্তি দরকার

Weak: 'Governments should invest in public transport.' (Just a suggestion — no explanation)

দুর্বল: 'Governments should invest in public transport.' (শুধু পরামর্শ — কোনো ব্যাখ্যা নেই)

Strong: 'Governments should invest in affordable public transport, as this would provide a viable alternative to private vehicles and reduce the number of cars on the road, directly addressing the congestion problem.'

শক্তিশালী: সমাধান + কেন এটি কাজ করবে + সমস্যার সাথে সংযোগ — এটি Task Response-এ উচ্চ নম্বর দেয়।

3a. Model question & full answer / মডেল প্রশ্ন ও পূর্ণ উত্তর

Model Question / মডেল প্রশ্ন

In many cities around the world, traffic congestion has become a serious problem. What are the main causes of this problem, and what measures could be taken to reduce it?

বিশ্বের অনেক শহরে যানজট একটি গুরুতর সমস্যা হয়ে উঠেছে। এই সমস্যার মূল কারণগুলো কী, এবং এটি কমাতে কী ব্যবস্থা নেওয়া যেতে পারে?

Full model answer (Band 7–8) / পূর্ণ মডেল উত্তর:

[Introduction] Traffic congestion has emerged as one of the most pressing challenges facing urban centres worldwide, with significant consequences for productivity, public health, and quality of life. Understanding the underlying causes of this problem is essential to identifying effective solutions.

[Body 1 — Causes] The primary cause of urban traffic congestion is the rapid increase in private car ownership, which has outpaced the development of supporting road infrastructure in most cities. As urban populations grow and incomes rise, more individuals purchase vehicles, resulting in a volume of traffic that existing road networks were never designed to accommodate. Compounding this is the widespread inadequacy of public transport alternatives in many cities: when bus and rail services are infrequent, unreliable, or expensive, commuters have little practical choice but to drive. In rapidly growing cities across Southeast Asia and South America, car ownership has doubled or tripled within a single decade, illustrating how quickly congestion can reach critical levels.

[Body 2 — Solutions] Several measures could effectively address this problem. Most significantly, governments should invest in expanding and subsidising public transport systems, making them a genuinely competitive alternative to private vehicles. When journeys by bus or train are faster, cheaper, and more convenient than driving, many commuters will voluntarily switch. Additionally, introducing congestion pricing — charging drivers a fee to enter high-traffic areas during peak hours — has proven effective in cities such as London and Singapore, where car use in central areas fell measurably following its introduction.

[Conclusion] In conclusion, traffic congestion is primarily the result of insufficient public transport combined with rising car ownership. A coordinated approach — expanding transit networks alongside economic incentives to reduce car use — offers the most sustainable path to alleviating this urban challenge.

(284 words)

4TWO-PART QUESTION ESSAY / দুই অংশের প্রশ্নের রচনা

A two-part question asks two separate, distinct questions — usually one about causes/reasons and one about effects/evaluation. Each body paragraph must address one question completely using PEEL. The most dangerous mistake is spending 80% of the essay on question 1 and only 20% on question 2.

Two-part question দুটি আলাদা, স্বতন্ত্র প্রশ্ন করে — সাধারণত একটি কারণ/কারণ সম্পর্কে এবং অন্যটি প্রভাব/মূল্যায়ন সম্পর্কে। প্রতিটি body paragraph PEEL ব্যবহার করে একটি প্রশ্নের সম্পূর্ণ উত্তর দিতে হবে। সবচেয়ে বিপজ্জনক ভুল হলো essay-এর ৮০% প্রশ্ন ১-এ এবং মাত্র ২০% প্রশ্ন ২-তে ব্যয় করা।

⚠️ The two-part balance rule / Two-part ভারসাম্যের নিয়ম

Each question gets approximately equal space — roughly the same number of sentences and the same level of development. Reading the question carefully is critical: 'Why is this?' needs causes. 'Is this positive or negative?' needs an evaluative judgement with evidence.

প্রতিটি প্রশ্ন প্রায় সমান স্থান পায় — প্রায় সমান সংখ্যক বাক্য এবং সমান স্তরের development। প্রশ্ন সতর্কতার সাথে পড়া গুরুত্বপূর্ণ: 'Why is this?' কারণ চায়। 'Is this positive or negative?' প্রমাণ সহ মূল্যায়নমূলক বিচার চায়।

4a. Model question & full answer / মডেল প্রশ্ন ও পূর্ণ উত্তর

Model Question / মডেল প্রশ্ন

In recent years, the number of people choosing to live alone has increased significantly in many countries. Why is this the case? Is this a positive or negative development?

সাম্প্রতিক বছরগুলোতে, অনেক দেশে একা বসবাস করতে পছন্দ করা মানুষের সংখ্যা উল্লেখযোগ্যভাবে বেড়েছে। এটি কেন হচ্ছে? এটি কি ইতিবাচক নাকি নেতিবাচক উন্নয়ন?

Full model answer (Band 7–8) / পূর্ণ মডেল উত্তর:

[Introduction] The significant rise in single-person households represents one of the most notable demographic shifts in modern society. This trend can be attributed to several interconnected social and economic factors, and its consequences for individuals and communities are complex.

[Body 1 — Why] The primary drivers of this trend are rising individual incomes and deep-rooted cultural shifts towards personal autonomy. As people earn more, solo living becomes financially viable — particularly in cities where a single income can support an independent lifestyle. Simultaneously, changing social attitudes have made living alone a socially accepted, even aspirational, choice rather than a marker of failure. Prolonged education and delayed marriage further extend the period during which young adults live independently. In Scandinavian countries, where individual incomes are highest and cultural emphasis on autonomy is strongest, rates of solo living are the highest in the world.

[Body 2 — Positive or negative] This development has both positive and negative dimensions, though on balance it is a mixed phenomenon. On the positive side, living alone allows individuals to develop self-reliance, organise their lives according to personal preferences, and build focused careers without domestic compromise. However, the negative consequences are significant. Single-person households consume disproportionately more energy and housing resources per capita than multi-person homes, contributing to environmental strain. More seriously, research consistently links living alone to higher rates of loneliness and social isolation, which are associated with long-term mental and physical health risks.

[Conclusion] In conclusion, the rise of solo living reflects genuine gains in individual freedom and economic independence, but it also poses real challenges for community cohesion and public health. Whether this is ultimately positive or negative depends significantly on the social support structures governments put in place to address the isolation it can generate.

(289 words)

Practice 2c — Advantages & Disadvantages (Remote work):

The ability to work remotely from any location has emerged as one of the most transformative developments in contemporary working life, accelerated by advances in digital technology. In my view, the advantages of this development outweigh the disadvantages.

The primary advantage of location-independent working is the flexibility it affords both employees and employers. For workers, the elimination of daily commutes reclaims significant time and reduces transport costs, improving both productivity and work-life balance. For companies, the ability to hire from a global talent pool means they are no longer restricted to candidates within commuting distance, enabling them to find the most suitable people for each role. Studies conducted in recent years consistently indicate that remote workers report higher job satisfaction and equal or superior productivity compared to their office-based counterparts.

However, remote working does carry genuine disadvantages. The boundary between professional and personal life can become dangerously blurred, leading to overwork and eventual burnout. Additionally, the informal mentoring and collaborative energy that develop naturally in shared physical spaces are difficult to replicate digitally, which may particularly disadvantage junior employees who rely on workplace proximity for professional development.

In conclusion, while remote work introduces real challenges around professional boundaries and mentoring, its gains in flexibility, talent access, and employee well-being represent a net benefit for most workers and organisations that manage its drawbacks thoughtfully.

(283 words)
Practice 3c — Problem & Solution (Student mental health):

The declining mental health of students has become an increasingly urgent concern for educational institutions worldwide. If left unaddressed, this issue carries serious consequences for both academic performance and long-term personal well-being.

Poor student mental health generates a range of interconnected problems. Most directly, it impairs academic performance: students experiencing anxiety, depression, or chronic stress struggle to concentrate, retain information, or meet deadlines effectively, which undermines their educational outcomes. Beyond the classroom, untreated mental health difficulties in young people are associated with social withdrawal and, in more serious cases, long-term psychological conditions that affect employment and relationships in adulthood. University counselling services in many countries have reported significant increases in demand for support, suggesting the scale of the problem is growing.

Several solutions could effectively address this issue. Educational institutions should introduce well-being programmes as a core part of the curriculum, teaching students to recognise stress, develop resilience, and seek help when needed. Equally important is ensuring that adequate professional support is easily accessible: schools and universities should employ sufficient numbers of trained counsellors and reduce the stigma around seeking mental health assistance. Institutions that have introduced compulsory well-being modules have reported measurable improvements in student outcomes.

In conclusion, poor student mental health is a serious problem requiring systemic responses. Preventative education combined with accessible professional support represents the most effective and sustainable approach for educational institutions.

Practice 4c — Two-part question (Delaying family formation):

Delaying family formation until the thirties is one of the most visible demographic trends in many developed nations, and it can be explained by a combination of economic pressures and shifting social values.

The most significant reason for this trend is the increasing financial barrier to starting a family. In many countries, the high cost of housing means that younger people require considerably longer to achieve the financial stability traditionally seen as a prerequisite for raising children. Simultaneously, extended periods of higher education and demanding career development have encouraged many individuals to prioritise professional establishment in their twenties before taking on family responsibilities. In countries such as South Korea, Japan, and much of Western Europe, the average age of first-time parents has risen by nearly a decade over the past forty years.

The societal effects of this trend are significant and largely negative at a collective level. Declining birth rates are producing ageing populations with shrinking workforces, placing increasing financial pressure on pension and healthcare systems. Japan presents a stark example: its rapidly ageing population has created severe economic challenges, including a shrinking labour market and rising public debt. At an individual level, however, older parenthood is associated with greater financial stability and emotional maturity, which may benefit children's development.

In conclusion, delayed family formation is a rational response to modern economic and social conditions, but its demographic consequences pose serious long-term challenges that governments must address through family-supportive housing, childcare, and parental leave policies.
