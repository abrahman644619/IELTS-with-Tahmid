How to use this worksheet / এই ওয়ার্কশিটটি যেভাবে ব্যবহার করবেন

This worksheet covers the three most specialised Task 1 types: Maps, Man-Made Processes, and Natural Processes. Each requires a completely different vocabulary bank and sentence style. Study one section at a time, understand the grammar rule (passive for man-made, active for natural), then practise under 20-minute exam conditions.

এই ওয়ার্কশিটে টাস্ক ১-এর তিনটি সবচেয়ে বিশেষায়িত টাইপ কভার করা হয়েছে: Maps, Man-Made Processes, এবং Natural Processes। প্রতিটির জন্য সম্পূর্ণ আলাদা vocabulary bank ও বাক্য-শৈলী প্রয়োজন। একটি করে section পড়ুন, grammar নিয়মটি বুঝুন (man-made-এর জন্য passive, natural-এর জন্য active), তারপর ২০ মিনিটের মধ্যে অনুশীলন করুন।

1MAPS / ম্যাপ

A map task shows you two plans of the same place at two different times (or before/after development). Your job is to describe the changes between them systematically. Unlike data charts, map tasks use no numbers — the language is entirely spatial and change-based.

Map task-এ একই স্থানের দুটি plan দুই ভিন্ন সময়ের দেখানো হয় (বা উন্নয়নের আগে/পরে)। আপনার কাজ হলো পার্থক্যগুলো পদ্ধতিগতভাবে বর্ণনা করা। Data chart-এর বিপরীতে, map task-এ কোনো সংখ্যা নেই — ভাষা সম্পূর্ণ spatial ও পরিবর্তন-ভিত্তিক।

🎯 5-step approach for map tasks / Map task-এর ৫-ধাপ পদ্ধতি

1. Identify what stayed the same (unchanged features go in your overview).

১. কী অপরিবর্তিত থেকেছে তা চিহ্নিত করুন (overview-এ যাবে)।

2. Group changes by area: north, south, east, west, centre.

২. পরিবর্তনগুলো এলাকা অনুযায়ী গ্রুপ করুন: north, south, east, west, centre।

3. For each change, use a change verb (was replaced by, was converted into, was demolished).

৩. প্রতিটি পরিবর্তনের জন্য change verb ব্যবহার করুন (was replaced by, was converted into, was demolished)।

4. Note any features added for the first time and note what is absent from the original.

৪. প্রথমবার যোগ হওয়া features উল্লেখ করুন এবং মূল map-এ কী ছিল না তা বলুন।

5. Keep your tense consistent: use the past passive (was built, was extended) throughout.

৫. Tense সামঞ্জস্যপূর্ণ রাখুন: পুরো লেখায় past passive (was built, was extended) ব্যবহার করুন।

⚠️ Critical grammar: In vs To / গুরুত্বপূর্ণ grammar: In বনাম To

"In the north" = a feature located INSIDE an area. Example: The station is in the north of the town.

"in the north" = একটি এলাকার ভেতরে অবস্থিত। উদাহরণ: The station is in the north of the town।

"To the north" = a feature located OUTSIDE, used for relative comparison. Example: The park is to the north of the market.

"to the north" = বাইরে অবস্থিত, তুলনামূলক ব্যবহারের জন্য। উদাহরণ: The park is to the north of the market।

In IELTS maps, features are usually inside the plan, so "in the north/south/east/west" is more common.

IELTS map-এ সাধারণত features plan-এর ভেতরে থাকে, তাই "in the north/south/east/west" বেশি ব্যবহার হয়।

Model Question / মডেল প্রশ্ন

The maps below show the town centre of Merford in 1990 and 2023. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant. Write at least 150 words.

নিচের map দুটি দেখাচ্ছে ১৯৯০ এবং ২০২৩ সালে Merford শহরের কেন্দ্র। মূল বৈশিষ্ট্যগুলো নির্বাচন ও উল্লেখ করে তথ্য সংক্ষেপ করুন।

Model Answer / মডেল উত্তর:

The maps illustrate how the town centre of Merford changed over a period of approximately thirty years, between 1990 and 2023.

Overall, the town underwent considerable development during this period, with several original features demolished or replaced and new infrastructure added to accommodate growth. The most significant changes occurred in the eastern and central areas.

In 1990, the eastern section of the town was occupied by a park, while the centre housed a market hall. By 2023, both had been replaced: the park gave way to a large shopping centre, and the market hall was converted into a pedestrian zone. In the northeast, a hotel was constructed — a feature entirely absent from the original map. The railway station in the north retained its original position throughout the period, though the residential area to the west was extended considerably into a larger housing development.

In the southern part of the town, the original car park was replaced by a multi-storey facility of considerably greater capacity. Additionally, a new bypass road was constructed along the southern edge of the town, diverting traffic away from the central area.

(190 words)

2MAN-MADE PROCESSES / মানব-সৃষ্ট প্রক্রিয়া

A man-made process diagram shows how humans manufacture, produce, or recycle something. It is always a linear sequence of steps (never a cycle). The defining grammatical feature is the PASSIVE VOICE: the material is acted upon, not acting.

Man-made process diagram দেখায় মানুষ কীভাবে কিছু তৈরি করে, উৎপাদন করে বা পুনর্ব্যবহার করে। এটি সবসময় linear ধারাবাহিক steps (কখনো cycle নয়)। সংজ্ঞায়িত grammatical feature হলো PASSIVE VOICE: উপাদানটির উপর কাজ করা হয়, উপাদানটি নিজে কাজ করে না।

🎯 The passive voice rule for man-made processes / Man-made process-এর passive voice নিয়ম

✓ CORRECT (passive): The paper is shredded. → The pulp is then filtered. → The sheets are dried.

✓ সঠিক (passive): The paper is shredded. / The pulp is then filtered. / The sheets are dried।

✗ WRONG (active): Workers shred the paper. → They filter the pulp.

✗ ভুল (active): Workers shred the paper. / They filter the pulp।

The subject of every sentence should be the MATERIAL being processed, not the people doing the processing.

প্রতিটি বাক্যের subject হবে যে MATERIAL প্রক্রিয়া করা হচ্ছে, কাজ করা মানুষ নয়।

Model Question / মডেল প্রশ্ন

The diagram below shows the process by which used paper is recycled to produce new paper products. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant. Write at least 150 words.

নিচের diagram দেখাচ্ছে ব্যবহৃত কাগজ কীভাবে পুনর্ব্যবহার করে নতুন কাগজ তৈরি করা হয়।

Model Answer / মডেল উত্তর:

The diagram illustrates the process by which used paper is recycled to produce new paper products.

Overall, the recycling process involves nine distinct stages, beginning with the collection of waste paper and culminating in a finished product that is ready for distribution.

In the first stage, used paper is collected from homes and businesses and transported to a recycling facility. Here, it is sorted according to paper grade before being fed into a shredding machine, where it is broken down into small fragments. The shredded material is then mixed with large quantities of water to form a thick pulp.

Once the pulp has been created, it passes through a filtering system, which removes contaminants such as staples and ink. The cleaned pulp is subsequently bleached to restore its whiteness. In the following stages, it is spread onto large rollers and pressed into flat sheets. These sheets are then dried in industrial ovens and, in the final stage, cut to the required size, producing finished paper ready for use.

(179 words)

3NATURAL PROCESSES / প্রাকৃতিক প্রক্রিয়া

A natural process shows how nature works without human intervention — the water cycle, life cycles, seasons, geological changes. Unlike man-made processes, natural processes use ACTIVE VOICE because the natural agents (the sun, water, animals) perform the actions themselves.

Natural process দেখায় মানুষের হস্তক্ষেপ ছাড়াই প্রকৃতি কীভাবে কাজ করে — water cycle, life cycle, ঋতু, ভূতাত্ত্বিক পরিবর্তন। Man-made process-এর বিপরীতে, natural process ACTIVE VOICE ব্যবহার করে কারণ প্রাকৃতিক কারক (সূর্য, পানি, প্রাণী) নিজেরাই কাজ করে।

🎯 Active vs Passive — The core rule / Active বনাম Passive — মূল নিয়ম

MAN-MADE PROCESS (passive): The water is heated. → The paper is shredded. → The material is filtered.

মানব-সৃষ্ট প্রক্রিয়া (passive): The water is heated. / The paper is shredded. / The material is filtered।

NATURAL PROCESS (active): The sun heats the ocean. → Water evaporates. → Clouds form.

প্রাকৃতিক প্রক্রিয়া (active): The sun heats the ocean. / Water evaporates. / Clouds form।

Quick test: Can you put 'by humans' at the end? If YES → passive. If NO → active.

Quick test: শেষে 'by humans' বসানো যায়? YES হলে → passive। NO হলে → active।

Model Question / মডেল প্রশ্ন

The diagram below illustrates the water cycle. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant. Write at least 150 words.

নিচের diagram দেখাচ্ছে water cycle। মূল বৈশিষ্ট্যগুলো নির্বাচন ও উল্লেখ করে তথ্য সংক্ষেপ করুন।

Model Answer / মডেল উত্তর:

The diagram illustrates the natural process by which water continuously circulates between the Earth's surface and the atmosphere, commonly known as the water cycle.

Overall, the process is driven primarily by solar energy and involves five interconnected stages: evaporation, condensation, precipitation, surface runoff, and groundwater percolation.

The cycle begins when solar heat warms the surface of oceans and lakes, causing water to evaporate and rise into the atmosphere as water vapour. As this vapour ascends to higher altitudes, it cools, condenses around tiny particles, and forms clouds. When sufficient moisture accumulates, it falls back to the Earth's surface as precipitation in the form of rain or snow.

Water that falls onto mountainous areas then flows downhill as surface runoff, collecting into rivers which eventually carry it back to the ocean, where the cycle begins again. Simultaneously, some precipitation seeps into the ground through percolation, replenishing underground aquifers and providing a secondary pathway through which water slowly returns to the surface.

(186 words)

Overall, Hadley experienced significant transformation, with agricultural land and traditional village features largely replaced by residential and commercial facilities. The only feature to remain unchanged was the church in the centre of the village.

Practice 2b — Man-made process (Olive oil production):

The diagram illustrates the process by which olives are transformed into olive oil.

Overall, the production process involves nine distinct stages, beginning with the harvesting of olives from trees and concluding with bottling and distribution to consumers.

In the first stage, olives are harvested and transported to a processing facility, where they are washed and cleaned to remove leaves and impurities. Once cleaned, the olives are fed into a mechanical crusher, where they are ground into a thick paste. The paste then undergoes a process known as malaxation, during which it is gently stirred for up to forty minutes to allow the oil droplets to combine.

After malaxation, the paste is placed in a centrifuge, which separates the olive oil from the remaining water and solids. The extracted oil is subsequently filtered to remove fine particles before being stored in large stainless steel tanks. In the final stage, the oil is bottled and prepared for distribution.

(180 words)
Practice 3b — Natural process (Butterfly life cycle):

The diagram illustrates the life cycle of a butterfly, a continuous process that consists of six distinct stages.

Overall, the butterfly undergoes a complete metamorphosis, transforming dramatically in physical form from egg to adult. Because the process is cyclical, it begins and ends with reproduction.

The cycle begins when an adult butterfly lays its eggs on the surface of leaves. After several days, the eggs hatch and tiny caterpillars, also known as larvae, emerge. The caterpillars feed voraciously on plant leaves, growing rapidly and shedding their outer skin several times as they increase in size.

Once a caterpillar has grown sufficiently, it attaches itself to a branch and forms a protective casing called a pupa, or chrysalis. Inside the pupa, a complex biological transformation takes place over a period of days or weeks. Eventually, a fully formed adult butterfly emerges, spreads its wings, and feeds on flower nectar. The adult then finds a mate, lays a new clutch of eggs, and the cycle begins again.

(184 words)
