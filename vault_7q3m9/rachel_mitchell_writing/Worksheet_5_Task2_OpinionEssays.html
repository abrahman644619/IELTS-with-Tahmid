How to use this worksheet / এই ওয়ার্কশিটটি যেভাবে ব্যবহার করবেন

This worksheet covers the two most common opinion-based Task 2 types: Argument-led (Discuss both views) and Thesis-led (Agree/disagree). For each type, you will study the structure, analyse a full model answer, build targeted vocabulary, and then write a complete timed essay. Set a timer for 40 minutes for each practice task.

এই ওয়ার্কশিটে Task 2-এর দুটি সবচেয়ে সাধারণ opinion-ভিত্তিক ধরন কভার করা হয়েছে: Argument-led (উভয় মত আলোচনা) এবং Thesis-led (একমত/দ্বিমত)। প্রতিটির জন্য আপনি কাঠামো অধ্যয়ন করবেন, একটি পূর্ণ model answer বিশ্লেষণ করবেন, vocabulary তৈরি করবেন, এবং তারপর সময়ের মধ্যে একটি সম্পূর্ণ essay লিখবেন। প্রতিটি practice task-এর জন্য ৪০ মিনিটের timer সেট করুন।

⚠️ The most common confusion / সবচেয়ে সাধারণ বিভ্রান্তি

Many candidates accidentally put their personal opinion in the body paragraphs of an Argument-led essay. In body paragraphs, ONLY impersonal language: 'Some people argue… / Critics contend… / Proponents maintain…'

অনেক শিক্ষার্থী ভুলে Argument-led essay-এর body paragraph-এ ব্যক্তিগত মত দেন। Body paragraph-এ শুধুমাত্র impersonal language: 'Some people argue… / Critics contend… / Proponents maintain…'

2ARGUMENT-LED ESSAY / Argument-led রচনা

You are a judge, not a lawyer. Present both sides of a debate fairly and equally. Reserve your personal opinion entirely for the conclusion. The examiner wants to see that you can consider multiple perspectives before forming a judgement.

আপনি একজন বিচারক, আইনজীবী নন। বিতর্কের উভয় দিক ন্যায্য ও সমানভাবে উপস্থাপন করুন। আপনার ব্যক্তিগত মত সম্পূর্ণভাবে conclusion-এর জন্য রেখে দিন। পরীক্ষক দেখতে চান যে একটি মতামত গঠনের আগে আপনি একাধিক দৃষ্টিভঙ্গি বিবেচনা করতে পারেন।

2a. Band 6 vs Band 7+ body paragraph — what is the difference? / Band 6 বনাম Band 7+ body paragraph

Band 6 paragraph ✗

Topic: Technology simplifies daily life.

"Technology has made life simpler. Nowadays, people use technology every day. For example, they use smartphones and computers. This shows that technology is helpful. Many people think technology is good because it makes things easier."

Problems: No explanation (WHY is life simpler?). Vague example. Circular reasoning. Only 52 words.

Band 7+ paragraph ✓

Topic: Technology simplifies daily life.

"Technology has undoubtedly simplified many aspects of daily life by removing practical barriers to communication and commerce. In the past, banking required a physical visit during business hours; today, consumers can manage their finances from any location at any time. For example, smartphone applications have made international transfers — once a time-consuming process — achievable in seconds. This demonstrates that, in practical terms, technology has genuinely improved efficiency for millions of people."

Strengths: [P] Clear topic sentence. [E] Specific explanation with before/after contrast. [E] Precise example. [L] Closing sentence links back to the overall argument.

2b. Model question & full answer / মডেল প্রশ্ন ও পূর্ণ উত্তর

Model Question / মডেল প্রশ্ন

Some people believe that social media has had a negative impact on communication skills, while others argue it has improved the way people connect with each other. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

কিছু মানুষ বিশ্বাস করেন যে social media যোগাযোগ দক্ষতায় নেতিবাচক প্রভাব ফেলেছে, অন্যরা মনে করেন এটি মানুষের সংযোগের উপায় উন্নত করেছে।

Full model answer (Band 7–8) / পূর্ণ মডেল উত্তর (Band 7–8):

[Introduction] The rise of social media platforms has fundamentally transformed how billions of people communicate in the modern era. While some argue these platforms have weakened interpersonal communication skills, others maintain they have created valuable new channels for human connection.

[Body 1] Those who believe social media has damaged communication argue that reliance on brief, informal digital messages has reduced people's capacity for sustained, meaningful face-to-face interaction. Critics contend that as individuals spend increasing amounts of time engaging through screens, their ability for in-depth verbal conversation, active listening, and non-verbal expression gradually erodes. For instance, it is widely observed that many young people who are highly proficient in digital communication still struggle with the extended verbal demands of professional interviews or academic presentations.

[Body 2] On the other hand, proponents of social media argue that these platforms have dramatically expanded people's ability to form and maintain relationships across geographical distances that were previously insurmountable. Families separated by migration, friends on different continents, and professional communities spanning multiple countries can now sustain connections that would have been effectively impossible a generation ago. A clear example is diaspora communities, which use video calling and messaging platforms to preserve cultural ties and family bonds across thousands of kilometres.

[Conclusion] In conclusion, whilst both perspectives contain genuine merit, I believe that social media's capacity to bridge physical distance and sustain human relationships represents a greater contribution to communication than the limitations it imposes. That said, developing traditional communication skills alongside digital ones remains important for well-rounded individuals.

(283 words)

3THESIS-LED ESSAY / Thesis-led রচনা

You are a lawyer, not a judge. You have already decided your position — now you must build the strongest possible case to support it. State your opinion clearly in the introduction and defend it through both body paragraphs. You may briefly acknowledge the other side, but always return to your position.

আপনি একজন আইনজীবী, বিচারক নন। আপনি ইতিমধ্যে আপনার অবস্থান নির্ধারণ করেছেন — এখন এটি সমর্থন করার জন্য সবচেয়ে শক্তিশালী যুক্তি তৈরি করতে হবে। Introduction-এ স্পষ্টভাবে মত বলুন এবং উভয় body paragraph-এ এটি সমর্থন করুন। সংক্ষেপে অন্য দিক স্বীকার করতে পারেন, কিন্তু সর্বদা নিজের অবস্থানে ফিরে আসুন।

🎯 The partial agreement strategy — your most flexible tool / Partial agreement কৌশল — সবচেয়ে নমনীয় হাতিয়ার

Most thesis-led questions allow you to partially agree. This is actually the safest and highest-scoring approach because:

অধিকাংশ thesis-led প্রশ্নে আপনি আংশিকভাবে একমত হতে পারেন। এটি সবচেয়ে নিরাপদ ও বেশি নম্বর-দেওয়া পদ্ধতি কারণ:

Body 1: Concede what is true on the other side ('Admittedly, X is true to some extent…')

Body 1: অন্য দিকে যা সত্য তা স্বীকার করুন ('Admittedly, X is true to some extent…')

Body 2: Explain why your position is stronger ('Nevertheless, I maintain that Y…')

Body 2: কেন আপনার অবস্থান শক্তিশালী তা ব্যাখ্যা করুন ('Nevertheless, I maintain that Y…')

Result: Your essay shows nuance + critical thinking — hallmarks of band 7+.

ফলাফল: আপনার essay nuance ও critical thinking দেখায় — band 7+-এর বৈশিষ্ট্য।

3a. Model question & full answer / মডেল প্রশ্ন ও পূর্ণ উত্তর

Model Question / মডেল প্রশ্ন

The increasing use of technology in classrooms is making teachers less important. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

শ্রেণিকক্ষে প্রযুক্তির ক্রমবর্ধমান ব্যবহার শিক্ষকদের কম গুরুত্বপূর্ণ করে তুলছে। আপনি কতটা একমত বা দ্বিমত?

Full model answer (Band 7–8) / পূর্ণ মডেল উত্তর:

[Introduction] Technology has become an increasingly central component of modern education, with digital tools now integrated into classroom teaching at almost every level. While I acknowledge that this development has transformed certain teaching practices, I strongly disagree that it reduces the importance of teachers — if anything, the role of skilled educators has become more essential than ever.

[Body 1] Firstly, teachers provide something that technology fundamentally cannot: the human capacity to inspire curiosity, build emotional resilience, and develop critical thinking. Digital tools deliver content efficiently, but they cannot adapt in real time to a student's emotional state, identify hidden misunderstandings, or offer the motivational support that determines whether a learner persists through difficulty. The value of a teacher lies not in transmitting information — something technology now does with remarkable efficiency — but in cultivating the deeper cognitive and social skills that define a capable individual.

[Body 2] Furthermore, the growing volume of online information has actually increased the need for educators who can teach students to evaluate, contextualise, and think critically about what they find. Without expert guidance, students risk becoming passive consumers of content rather than independent thinkers. Evidence from high-performing education systems such as Finland and Singapore strongly suggests that sustained investment in teacher quality — not the replacement of teachers with technology — is the defining factor in educational excellence.

[Conclusion] In conclusion, while technology undoubtedly transforms the nature of classroom teaching, it complements rather than diminishes the educator's role. The most effective learning environments are those in which skilled teachers harness digital tools purposefully, guiding students towards deeper understanding.

(287 words)

Practice 2d — Argument-led (Public transport vs road networks):

Investment in urban infrastructure is a subject of considerable debate among policymakers and citizens alike. While some argue that expanding public transport networks should be the primary focus of government spending, others maintain that improving road infrastructure for private vehicles is the higher priority.

Proponents of public transport investment argue that well-developed rail and bus systems offer benefits extending beyond individual convenience. By providing affordable, reliable mobility for citizens who cannot afford private vehicles, robust public transport reduces social inequality and improves access to employment and healthcare. Moreover, at a time of growing environmental concern, shifting journeys from private cars to mass transit significantly reduces carbon emissions. Cities such as Tokyo and Amsterdam demonstrate that investment in world-class public transport can dramatically reduce car dependency while improving quality of life.

On the other hand, advocates of road investment point out that private vehicles offer a level of flexibility that public transport cannot match, particularly in rural and suburban areas. Improved road networks also directly benefit emergency services, freight logistics, and commercial activity, generating economic returns that extend well beyond commuting. For communities in remote areas, reliable road connections may represent the difference between functional access to essential services and genuine isolation.

In conclusion, while both positions have legitimate foundations, I believe that investment in public transport produces more equitable and environmentally sustainable outcomes for society as a whole. Governments should prioritise solutions that benefit the maximum number of citizens and address urgent environmental challenges.

Practice 3c — Thesis-led (Young people's independence):

The degree of independence exhibited by young people compared to previous generations is widely debated. I agree with this view to a considerable extent, though I believe this shift reflects broader structural changes in society rather than a fundamental failure of character.

There is meaningful evidence that traditional markers of independence are being achieved later by younger generations. In many countries, home ownership, full financial independence, and stable long-term employment — milestones that previous generations often reached in their early twenties — are now commonly delayed into the thirties. Additionally, the availability of technological conveniences that handle navigation, scheduling, and even financial management may reduce the need for young people to develop certain practical life skills independently. These patterns are well-documented across developed economies and represent a genuine shift in the timing of independence.

Nevertheless, it would be inaccurate to conclude that today's young people lack capability. Many demonstrate independence in ways their predecessors could not: founding global companies in their twenties, building international careers remotely, and navigating complex digital and social landscapes. Furthermore, the extended education that delays traditional independence also equips young people with analytical and professional capabilities that previous generations rarely possessed.

In conclusion, whilst certain forms of independence have declined, today's young people exhibit new capabilities that were simply unavailable to earlier generations. A balanced assessment must acknowledge both the changes and the continuities rather than drawing simplistic conclusions.

(282 words)
