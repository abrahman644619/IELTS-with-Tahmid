শেখার লক্ষ্য | Learning Objectives

এই worksheet শেষ করলে তুমি পারবে —

  • Read and interpret data from a pie chart.
  • Use key vocabulary for describing percentages: make up, represent, account for, per cent, proportion, respectively.
  • Use popular and famous correctly in sentences.
  • Form comparatives: bigger than, more popular than, less expensive than.
  • Form superlatives: the biggest, the most popular, the least popular.
  • Vary the subject of sentences when describing chart data.
  • Structure a Task 1 answer using three parts: topic sentence, overview, and data description.
  • Write a guided paragraph describing a pie chart.

5B — 'Popular' vs 'Famous' | পার্থক্য দেখো

গুরুত্বপূর্ণ পার্থক্য — এই দুটো শব্দ এক নয়!

POPULAR = অনেক মানুষ পছন্দ করে বা ব্যবহার করে। | Many people like or choose it.

FAMOUS = অনেক মানুষ জানে বা শুনেছে। | Many people have heard about it, but they may not like it.

Examples: Hamburgers are popular in the USA (many people eat them). McDonald's is famous all over the world (people know about it, but they may or may not eat there).

Grammar Focus | Comparatives & Superlatives

IELTS Task 1-এ pie chart বা graph বর্ণনা করার সময় তুলনা করা খুবই জরুরি। এই section-এ তুমি comparatives ও superlatives লিখবে।

Grammar Note | ব্যাকরণ নোট

COMPARATIVE — দুটি জিনিসের মধ্যে তুলনা করতে:

  • One-syllable adj (ছোট adj): adjective + -er + than → big → bigger than, high → higher than
  • Two+ syllable adj (বড় adj): more + adjective + than → popular → more popular than
  • Negative comparison: less + adjective + than → sushi is less popular than pizza

SUPERLATIVE — একটি group-এর মধ্যে সর্বোচ্চ বা সর্বনিম্ন:

  • One-syllable adj: the + adjective + -est → big → the biggest
  • Two+ syllable adj: the most + adjective → popular → the most popular
  • Negative superlative: the least + adjective → sushi → the least popular

Language Focus | বাক্যের Subject পরিবর্তন করো

IELTS Task 1-এ একটি ভালো paragraph লিখতে হলে subject পরিবর্তন করা জরুরি — একই subject বারবার ব্যবহার করলে লেখা একঘেয়ে হয়।

তুমি তিনটি ভিন্ন subject ব্যবহার করতে পারো:

Subject 1: The type of fast food → Hamburgers are the most popular fast food in the survey.

Subject 2: The percentage of teenagers → 33 per cent of teenagers choose hamburgers.

Subject 3: The percentage as a proportion → Hamburgers account for the largest proportion of the pie chart.

The pie chart shows the fruit preferences of primary school children in New Zealand. In general, bananas and strawberries are the most popular fruits, while kiwi fruit is the least popular. Teacher Notes

Unit 1 — Fast Food: Teaching Tips & Notes

  • This worksheet is designed for IELTS Task 1 (pie chart description). Suitable after Unit 0 is completed.
  • Section 3 (Warm-Up): Do this orally first — ask students to name fast foods they know. This activates schema about food preferences and survey data.
  • Section 4 (Chart Reading): The pie chart data is presented as a visual. If you have a projector, draw a rough pie chart on the board using the percentages — this visual connection is important.
  • Section 5B (Popular vs Famous): Common error — Bangladeshi learners often use 'famous' when they mean 'popular'. Emphasise with local examples (e.g. 'Naan bread is popular in Bangladesh' vs 'The Shahid Minar is famous in Bangladesh').
  • Section 5C (Vocabulary Fill): Note that 'make up', 'represent', and 'account for' are interchangeable in meaning but stylistically distinct. Encourage students to vary their use.
