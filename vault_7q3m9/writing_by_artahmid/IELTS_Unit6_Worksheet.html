Grammar Focus 1 | Comparatives & Superlatives with Nouns
Grammar Note | ব্যাকরণ নোট

With ADJECTIVES:

  • One-syllable adj: adj + -er + than / the + adj + -est → bigger than / the biggest
  • Two+ syllable adj: more/less + adj + than / the most/least + adj → more expensive than / the least expensive

With NOUNS:

  • Uncountable nouns: more/less + noun / the most/least + noun → more money / the least money
  • Countable plural nouns: more/fewer + noun / the most/fewest + noun → fewer expenses / the fewest billboards

Key rule: 'less/least' → uncountable. 'fewer/fewest' → countable plural.

Language Focus 3 | Contrast Linking Words | Contrast বোঝানোর শব্দ
Grammar Note | ব্যাকরণ নোট

Two types of contrast linkers:

  • WITHIN a sentence: while / whereas → used to join two clauses in ONE sentence.
  • AT THE START of a new sentence: Conversely, / In contrast, → starts a new sentence.

while — Acme spent $125k on newspapers, while Famous spent only $75k.

whereas — Acme spent more on newspapers, whereas Famous spent more on the Internet.

Conversely, — Acme increased its advertising budget. Conversely, Famous reduced theirs.

In contrast, — Acme dominated newspaper advertising. In contrast, Famous dominated Internet spending.

Key rule: 'while/whereas' joins two ideas in ONE sentence. 'Conversely/In contrast' starts a NEW sentence.

Teacher Notes

Unit 6 — Advertising: Teaching Tips

  • This worksheet includes 3 grouped bar charts with annotated key trends — Charts 1 and 2 show two-company comparisons; Chart 3 shows a two-age-group comparison.
  • Section 3 (Warm-Up): Q3 asks students to look at Chart 1 and write sentences BEFORE reading the model. This 'notice first' approach is powerful — it activates language and makes Model 1 more meaningful.
  • Section 5B (Irregular Plurals): Drill these as a class list — say the singular, students call out the plural. Focus on 'data', 'media', 'criteria' as they are very common IELTS vocabulary items.
  • Section 6A ('respectively'): Emphasise the pattern — [A] and [B] + [figure A] and [figure B] respectively. Common error: reversing the order of figures. Practise by asking students to read 2 figures back in order.
  • Section 7A (Comparatives/Superlatives): Do items a and b as class examples. Focus on the 'money' vs 'time' cue — uncountable → more/less; countable → more/fewer.
  • Section 8 (Quantifying): Introduce 'X times as much/many as' only AFTER students are confident with comparatives. Common error: 'Famous spent twice more than Acme' — wrong. Correct: 'Famous spent twice as much as Acme'.
  • Section 9B (Contrast Sentences): Ask students to categorise each sentence as 'within sentence' or 'new sentence' before rewriting. This builds metacognitive awareness.
  • Section 10B (Framework): Allow students to complete this semi-independently before revealing Model 2. This scaffolds the transition from guided to independent writing.
  • Section 11 (Writing Practice): Collect and mark using IELTS Task 1 criteria. Bonus marks for using all four contrast linking words AND at least two 'X times as much/many' sentences.
