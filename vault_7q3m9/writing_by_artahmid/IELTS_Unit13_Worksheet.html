এই worksheet-টি IELTS Writing বইয়ের Unit 13 (Technological Processes) অনুযায়ী তৈরি। এটি IELTS Task 1-এর উপর — যেখানে তোমাকে একটি প্রক্রিয়া চিত্র (process diagram) দেখে একটি paragraph লিখতে হবে।
Process diagram সাধারণত কোনো কিছু তৈরির ধাপগুলো দেখায়। এই unit-এ তুমি শিখবে: (১) প্যাসিভ ভয়েস দিয়ে প্রতিটি ধাপ বর্ণনা করতে, (২) উদ্দেশ্য বোঝাতে 'to + verb' ব্যবহার করতে, (৩) প্রতিটি ধাপ শুরু করতে prepositional phrases ব্যবহার করতে।

প্রযুক্তিগত প্রক্রিয়া ও খাদ্য উৎপাদন — এই বিষয়গুলো ইসলামিক দৃষ্টিকোণ থেকেও গুরুত্বপূর্ণ। আল্লাহ মানুষকে 'আকল' (বুদ্ধি ও জ্ঞান) দিয়েছেন — এই জ্ঞান ব্যবহার করে আমরা হালাল ও পবিত্র খাদ্য নিশ্চিত করতে পারি। এই unit পড়তে পড়তে সেই দায়িত্ব মনে রেখো।

Model 1 — Ice Cream Making Process Description

The ice cream making process has six key stages. In the first stage, sugar, cream, and milk are mixed together in a 3,000 gallon vat to make the basic ice cream mix.

In Stage Two, this mix is pasteurised, or heated to 70 degrees centigrade for 20 seconds, to kill any bacteria.

After that, flavour such as chocolate, vanilla, or strawberry is added and mixed using steel blades.

In the fourth stage, the mix is frozen in a tube to -10 degrees centigrade, and at the same time, it is whipped by blades to prevent ice crystals from forming.

Then, the ice cream is packed into boxes and finally stored in freezers.

Grammar Focus 1 | The Passive Voice
Grammar Note | ব্যাকরণ নোট

Process বর্ণনায় আমরা passive voice ব্যবহার করি কারণ কে কাজটি করছে তা গুরুত্বপূর্ণ নয় — কী ঘটছে সেটাই গুরুত্বপূর্ণ।

গঠন (Structure): Subject + is / are + Past Participle (V3)

  • mix → Sugar and milk are mixed together. (চিনি ও দুধ মেলানো হয়।)
  • heat → The mix is heated to 70°C. (মিশ্রণটি 70°C-এ গরম করা হয়।)
  • pack → The ice cream is packed into boxes. (আইসক্রিম বাক্সে ভরা হয়।)

Note: "is" = singular subject (একটি জিনিস)  |  "are" = plural subject (একাধিক জিনিস)

Grammar Focus 2 | Infinitive with 'to' for Purpose
Grammar Note | ব্যাকরণ নোট

কোনো কাজের উদ্দেশ্য বা কারণ বলতে আমরা 'to' + base verb ব্যবহার করি। প্রশ্ন: কেন? (Why?) → উত্তর: to + verb...

গঠন (Structure): [Passive step] + to + [base verb] + [object]

  • The mix is heated to 70°C to kill any bacteria. (ব্যাকটেরিয়া মারার জন্য গরম করা হয়।)
  • The mix is whipped to prevent ice crystals from forming. (বরফ কণা রোধ করতে ফেটানো হয়।)
  • A starter is added to ripen the milk. (দুধ পাকানোর জন্য starter যোগ করা হয়।)
7A — Join Sentence Pairs Using 'to' | বাক্য জোড়া লাগাও

Join each pair of sentences using 'to + infinitive'. The action always comes first, then the purpose. The first one is done for you. (প্রতিটি জোড়া 'to + verb' দিয়ে এক বাক্যে লেখো।)

Example | উদাহরণ

The cream is whipped. Air is introduced into the mixture.
→ The cream is whipped to introduce air into the mixture.

a. The flour and water are kneaded for a few minutes. | The dough becomes smooth and shiny.
b. The ice cream is put into boxes. | It is sold in shops.
c. The mixture is whipped with giant paddles. | Ice crystals are prevented from forming.
d. Chocolate is added to the mix. | The mix tastes very good.
e. The mix is heated to 70°C for twenty seconds. | The bacteria are killed.
f. The water is boiled for ten minutes. | The germs and bacteria are killed.
g. The ice cream is stored in the freezer. | It is prevented from melting and deteriorating.

Language Focus | Prepositional Phrases
Grammar Note | ব্যাকরণ নোট

ইংরেজিতে প্রায়ই বাক্যের শুরুতে prepositional phrase ব্যবহার করা হয় — কখন, কীভাবে বা কোন ক্রমে তা বোঝাতে। কিন্তু এই phrase-গুলো বাক্যের subject নয় — তাই এদের পরে অবশ্যই subject + verb আসতে হবে।

✗ Wrong: In the first stage, is mixed together. ← Subject নেই!

✓ Correct: In the first stage, sugar and milk are mixed together. ← Subject আছে ✓

Common process phrases: In the first/second/final stage, | In Stage Two/Three, | After that, | After + [gerund], | At the same time, | Then, | Finally,

Model 2 — Cheese Making Process Description

The cheese making process has five key stages. In the first stage, warm milk is cooled to 4°C in a refrigerated vat.

In Stage Two, this cooled milk is pasteurised, or heated to 63°C for 30 minutes, to kill any bacteria.

After that, the milk is cooled to 30°C and pumped into a cheese vat, where a starter is added to ripen the milk.

After 30 to 60 minutes, a coagulant such as rennet is added to separate the milk into the whey, which rises to the top, and the curds, which remain at the bottom of the vat.

Unit 13 — Technological Processes: Teaching Tips

  • This worksheet covers Task 1 Process Diagram description — less common in IELTS than graphs, but worth solid preparation. Focus the majority of class time on Grammar Focus 1 (Passive Voice, sections 6A–6B), as it is the single most important structure. Every stage of a process must be described in passive voice.
  • Three process flow diagrams are embedded: ice cream (6 stages, Model 1), cheese (5 stages, Model 2), and bread (6 stages, Writing Practice). When introducing each diagram, ask students to scan the boxes first and attempt to describe each stage orally before reading any model.
  • Bengali explanations in Grammar Notes use informal 'তুমি' throughout. This is intentional — the register matches how students are used to being addressed in a classroom. Do not change to formal 'আপনি' as it changes the tone significantly.
  • For Grammar Focus 1 (6B — Active to Passive): the most effective classroom strategy is to get students to identify the OBJECT of the active sentence first, then make it the SUBJECT of the passive. Drill this pattern visibly on the board before students attempt independently.
  • Word Forms table (5B): note that 'pasteurisation' (noun) is a technical term students often misspell. Drill the -tion suffix as a unit: pasteur-ise → pasteur-is-ation. Also note 'prevent' → 'preventive' (adjective) is irregular — most students write 'prevented'. Spend 2 minutes on this.
  • Section 7B (Shingara paragraph): this replaces the original Chinese dumplings text with a culturally familiar Bangladeshi halal food. Accept any grammatically correct purpose phrase — the goal is practising 'to + infinitive', not a fixed answer.
  • Language Focus 8B (error correction): missing subjects after prepositional phrases is the single most common error in Bangladeshi IELTS process writing. Write sentences 2, 4, 5, and 7 on the board and ask students to identify 'what is missing' before they write corrections. Metacognitive awareness is more durable than drill alone.
  • Section 9B (Guided Writing — gap-fill): give students 10 minutes to complete the gap-fill before revealing Model 2. Even incorrect answers generate engagement. After comparing, ask: 'Which of your words were different? Were they also correct?'
  • Writing Practice 10A (bread making): treat this as a timed exercise where possible — 5 minutes planning using the diagram, 20 minutes writing. The self-check cell at the end is designed to be used BEFORE submission. Train students to use it routinely; this habit directly improves Task 1 scores.
  • Islamic Note: The food manufacturing processes in this unit — ice cream, cheese, and bread — connect to the Islamic concept of Ni'mah (نعمة — divine bounty). Processes such as pasteurisation reflect humanity's use of 'Aql (عقل — God-given reason) to ensure our food is Tayyib (طيب — pure and wholesome), as commanded in Quran 2:168. The Prophet ﷺ said: 'There is no disease that Allah has created, except that He has also created its treatment.' Scientific knowledge applied to food safety is a form of fulfilling our Amanah (trust) as stewards of Allah's blessings. Encourage students to see the connection between this unit and their responsibility toward halal and wholesome living.
