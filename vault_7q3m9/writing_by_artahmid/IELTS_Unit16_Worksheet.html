শেখার লক্ষ্য | Learning Objectives

এই worksheet শেষ করলে তুমি পারবে —

  • Read and compare data from two pie charts showing the same range of categories.
  • Use 16 key vocabulary words for describing production, commodities, and proportions.
  • Describe pie chart segments using expressions: a significant percentage, over two thirds, less than one tenth, almost one third, about half, just over 20 per cent, etc.
  • Compare and contrast data using: In contrast, On the other hand, whereas, however, almost the same as.
  • Avoid repetition using reference words (it, its, they) and by removing repeated words.
  • Write a full Task 1 paragraph comparing two pie charts.

Model 1 — Metal Commodities: Austani and Kizani (2006)

The pie charts show 2006 production for two countries over the same range of five metal commodities. In general, Austani had a significant percentage of its production in iron ore, copper, and uranium, whereas Kizani had a more balanced output overall, although its biggest percentage of metal production was gold.

Austani had over two thirds of its production in iron ore and copper, at 41 per cent and 30 per cent respectively. Uranium contributed a further 20 per cent. Gold and silver, however, were relatively insignificant, equalling less than one tenth of the total.

In contrast, Kizani had a much more balanced output of metal commodities. Iron ore, uranium, and silver each made up 19 per cent. Gold, on the other hand, comprised almost one third of the overall production, which was a much higher percentage than in Austani. In contrast, copper output was much lower than Austani, at 12 per cent as against 30 per cent.

Essential Grammar | Describing Pie Chart Segments
Pie Chart Segment বর্ণনার Expressions | How to Describe Proportions

Pie chart বর্ণনায় আমরা proportions, ratios, percentages, fractions এবং shares ব্যবহার করতে পারি।

এই expressions-গুলো মনে রাখো — এগুলোর পরে সাধারণত "of" preposition আসে:

  • a significant percentage of → একটি উল্লেখযোগ্য শতাংশ
  • over two thirds of → দুই-তৃতীয়াংশের বেশি
  • a further X per cent → আরও X শতাংশ (adding to what was mentioned)
  • less than one tenth of → দশ ভাগের এক ভাগেরও কম
  • almost one third of → প্রায় এক-তৃতীয়াংশ
  • just over 20 per cent of → সামান্য বেশি ২০ শতাংশ
  • about half of → প্রায় অর্ধেক
  • a substantial proportion of → একটি উল্লেখযোগ্য অনুপাত

Note: "almost the same as" is the exception — it uses "as", not "of".

Comparison and Contrast Language | তুলনার ভাষা

দুটি pie chart বর্ণনায় আমরা মিল (similarity) এবং পার্থক্য (difference) উভয়ই বর্ণনা করি।

  • In contrast, Kizani had a much more balanced output... (বৈসাদৃশ্য দেখাতে)
  • On the other hand, Gold, on the other hand, comprised almost one third... (বৈসাদৃশ্য)
  • whereas Austani had a significant percentage..., whereas Kizani had a more balanced output... (বৈসাদৃশ্য — একই বাক্যে)
  • however Gold and silver, however, were relatively insignificant... (বিপরীত তথ্য যোগ করতে)
  • almost the same as Laptops and PCs were almost the same... (মিল দেখাতে)
  • as against copper output was much lower..., at 12 per cent as against 30 per cent... (সরাসরি তুলনা)

Avoiding Repetition | পুনরাবৃত্তি এড়ানো
পুনরাবৃত্তি এড়ানোর দুটি উপায় | Two Ways to Avoid Repetition

IELTS Task 1-এ একই তথ্য বারবার লেখা খুব খারাপ style। পুনরাবৃত্তি এড়াতে দুটি উপায় আছে:

উপায় ১ — Reference words ব্যবহার করো: it, they, them, its, their, this, that
উদাহরণ: Austani's production was high → Its production was high.

উপায় ২ — পুনরাবৃত্তিমূলক শব্দ বাদ দাও: Use "respectively" or simply remove repeated words.
উদাহরণ: Austani had 41% of Austani's production in iron ore, and Austani had 30% of Austani's production in copper.
→ Austani had 41% in iron ore and 30% in copper respectively.

In general, Siliconia had a significant proportion of its production in the PC, laptop, and notebook market, whereas Micronia produced a larger proportion of microchips for small electronic devices.

Over half of Siliconia's output was in PC microchips, at 55%. Microchips for laptops and notebooks made up another 35% of its output. Only one tenth of its total production was designed for PDAs, cell phones, and MP3 players.

In contrast, Micronia focused on microchips for small electronic devices such as PDAs, cell phones, and MP3 players. These three products together comprised a substantial proportion of the total output, at 30%, 25%, and 24% respectively. Microchips for notebooks were the next biggest category, at 14%. Laptops and PCs were almost the same, at 4% and 3% respectively.
