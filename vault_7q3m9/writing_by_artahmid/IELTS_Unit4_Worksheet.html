Model 1 — Topic: Problems of playing computer games

Playing computer games for a long time, either individually or with friends, causes a lot of problems. One major issue is the tendency for people to play for hours and neglect their studies. If they stay up very late, they will have trouble getting up in the morning for school or work. Another issue is the lack of exercise because the players spend so much time sitting down. If teenagers waste a lot of time playing on the computer and do no exercise, this can lead to obesity and health problems. In addition, spending hours using the console controls and making repetitive movements many times can cause eye strain or muscle problems.

Grammar Focus 1 | Verb + Verb-ing Pattern
Grammar Note | ব্যাকরণ নোট

ইংরেজিতে একটি সাধারণ verb pattern হলো: verb + another verb with -ing.

Pattern: [Main Verb] + [Verb-ing]

  • spend / waste time doing → He spends a lot of time watching TV.
  • have trouble doing → They have trouble getting up for school.
  • prefer / like / hate / love doing → I prefer playing computer games to swimming.
  • end up doing → Teenagers end up neglecting their studies.

Important: After these verbs, ALWAYS use -ing. NEVER use 'to + base verb'.

Grammar Focus 2 | Cause & Effect Linking Words
Grammar Note | ব্যাকরণ নোট

Problems essay লেখার সময় কারণ (cause) ও ফলাফল (effect) লেখাতে এই তিনটি linking word ব্যবহার করো:

  • (to) cause [effects] → কারণ → ফলাফল। Cause হলো subject। → Computer games can cause health problems.
  • (to) lead to [effects] → কারণ → ফলাফল। Cause হলো subject। → This can lead to eye strain.
  • (to) be due to [causes] → ফলাফল → কারণ। Effect হলো subject। → Muscle strain can be due to repetitive movements.

Important rule: All three are followed by a NOUN or NOUN PHRASE — NOT a full clause.

✔ Correct: This can lead to obesity. (noun)

✘ Wrong: This can lead to teenagers become obese. (NOT a noun phrase)

Grammar Focus 3 | Referencing Pronouns — they, this, it
Grammar Note | ব্যাকরণ নোট

'they', 'this', এবং 'it' ব্যবহার করলে একই শব্দ বারবার লেখার দরকার হয় না। এটি paragraph-কে coherent করে তোলে।

  • they → refers to people (পূর্বে উল্লিখিত ব্যক্তিরা) → Teenagers play games all night. They then have trouble waking up.
  • this → refers to an idea or action (পূর্বে উল্লিখিত ধারণা বা কাজ) → They post photos online. This can lead to privacy problems.
  • it → refers to a thing or situation (বিষয় বা পরিস্থিতি) → Gaming is addictive. It can cause students to neglect their studies.

Teacher Notes

Unit 4 — Youth Issues: Computer Use: Teaching Tips

  • This is the second Task 2 unit. Students should already know basic essay structure from Unit 2. Focus this unit on the 'problems' essay sub-type and cause-effect structure.
  • Section 3 (Warm-Up): The 4th question (parental control) can be briefly discussed as a class. This raises Islamic digital ethics — a valuable moment for values-based discussion.
  • Section 6 (Verb+ing): Drill the four key expressions — spend time doing, have trouble doing, end up doing, prefer/like doing — separately before doing the exercises. Write them on the board.
  • Section 6B (Choose Correct Words): This also reviews countable/uncountable nouns from Unit 2. Remind students: 'time' (uncountable), 'times' (countable — occasions), 'program' (countable).
  • Section 6C (Paragraph): Do the first 3 gaps as a class, then let students complete independently. This bridges controlled practice to guided writing.
  • Section 7 (Cause-Effect): Emphasise the crucial rule — cause/lead to/be due to are all followed by a NOUN or NOUN PHRASE. A common error is writing: 'This can lead to teenagers spend too much time…' — wrong. Correct: 'This can lead to teenagers spending…' or better: 'This can lead to a lack of study time.'
  • Section 8 (Referencing Pronouns): Ask students to physically underline the repeated words in 8B before they rewrite. This step helps them see what to replace.
  • Section 9 (Sentence Ordering): This is a critical paragraph structure skill. Do it collaboratively the first time — discuss why each sentence goes where it does.
  • Section 10 (Guided Writing): Mark strictly for: (1) cause-effect linking words, (2) verb+ing pattern, (3) referencing pronouns, (4) paragraph coherence.
