Task 2 Essay Structure | Essay-এর সঠিক গঠন

IELTS Task 2-এ সবসময় একটি নির্দিষ্ট গঠন মেনে essay লিখতে হয়। নিচের টেবিলটি ভালো করে পড়ো।

Part of EssayWhat to WriteApprox. Length
IntroductionIntroduce the topic. State what the essay will discuss. (বিষয়টি পরিচয় করাও। Essay কী নিয়ে হবে বলো।)2–3 sentences
Body Paragraph 1First main point + reason + example. Signpost: Firstly … (প্রথম মূল কারণ + ব্যাখ্যা + উদাহরণ।)4–6 sentences
Body Paragraph 2Second main point + reason + example. Signpost: Secondly … (দ্বিতীয় মূল কারণ + ব্যাখ্যা + উদাহরণ।)4–6 sentences

Model Text Reading | Model Text পড়ো ও বোঝো

নিচের Model paragraph-টি মনোযোগ দিয়ে পড়ো। এটি IELTS Task 2-এর একটি body paragraph-এর উদাহরণ।

Model 1 — Body Paragraph (Benefits of Team Sports)

Doing team sports is popular all over the world, and participation in sporting activities, for example, soccer or basketball, has many benefits. Firstly, sport promotes the development of the body and muscles and helps to keep the players fit. Secondly, this increase in physical fitness enables players to avoid obesity and health problems, like heart disease and high blood pressure. In addition, sportspeople develop useful group skills, such as teamwork and strategies. These skills can help them in their later lives and also in their jobs. Finally, people often do sport for social reasons, as it is a good way of making new friends and enjoying activities together.

Grammar Focus | Countable & Uncountable Nouns

IELTS Task 2-এ nouns সঠিকভাবে ব্যবহার করা খুবই জরুরি। Countable ও uncountable noun-এর মধ্যে পার্থক্য না জানলে অনেক ভুল হবে।

Grammar Note | ব্যাকরণ নোট
COUNTABLE NOUNS (গণনাযোগ্য বিশেষ্য):

গণনা করা যায়। Singular-এ 'a/an' ব্যবহার করো। Plural-এ -s/-es যোগ করো।

Examples: a skill → skills, a muscle → muscles, a strategy → strategies, a competition → competitions

UNCOUNTABLE NOUNS (অগণনাযোগ্য বিশেষ্য):

গণনা করা যায় না। 'a/an' ব্যবহার করা যায় না। Plural হয় না (-s যোগ হয় না)।

Examples: fitness, teamwork, obesity, participation, health, disease, blood pressure

SPECIAL RULE:

Words ending in -ing are often used as uncountable nouns in English.

Ex: 'Doing sport is good for you.' (NOT 'A doing sport')

Language Focus | Signpost Words — ধারণা যুক্তকারী শব্দ

IELTS Task 2-এ body paragraph লেখার সময় signpost words ব্যবহার করলে ideas সুন্দরভাবে সাজানো যায় এবং paragraph-টি পড়তে সহজ হয়।

Signpost Words — কখন ব্যবহার করবে?
  • Firstly, প্রথম কারণ বা সুবিধা শুরু করার জন্য। → Firstly, sport promotes the development of muscles.
  • Secondly, দ্বিতীয় কারণ শুরু করার জন্য। → Secondly, this increase in fitness helps avoid obesity.
  • In addition, আরও একটি কারণ যোগ করার জন্য। → In addition, sportspeople develop useful group skills.
  • Furthermore, অতিরিক্ত তথ্য যোগ করার জন্য। → Furthermore, sport also has social benefits.
  • Finally, শেষ কারণ বা সামাজিক সুবিধার জন্য। → Finally, sport is a good way of making new friends.
  • For example, উদাহরণ দেওয়ার জন্য। → For example, soccer and basketball require teamwork.
  • Such as, নির্দিষ্ট উদাহরণ দেওয়ার জন্য। → …skills such as teamwork and strategies.
  • As a result, কারণ ও ফলাফল দেখানোর জন্য। → As a result, players become fitter and healthier.

Guided Writing

এখন তুমি নিজে একটি IELTS Task 2 body paragraph লিখবে। নিচের topic ও notes দেখো।

Sentence starters এবং notes দেওয়া আছে — কিন্তু নিজের মতো করে সম্পূর্ণ করো। শুধু copy করো না।

Writing Topic (Task 2 Question):

Some people say that doing a team sport, such as soccer or basketball, should be compulsory for students. What are the benefits of team sports for high school students?

Your task: Write ONE body paragraph describing the benefits of team sports for students.

Planning Notes | লেখার আগে পরিকল্পনা করো

Think of 3–4 benefits of team sports for students. Use these ideas to help you:

  • Physical benefit: promotes development of muscles / increases fitness / helps avoid obesity and heart disease / controls blood pressure
  • Mental benefit: reduces stress / improves concentration / builds confidence
  • Social benefit: develops teamwork skills / builds friendships / teaches strategies / encourages cooperation
  • Life skills: teamwork, leadership, communication — useful in later life and in jobs
Sentence Starters (সব ব্যবহার করতে হবে না)
  • Doing team sports has many benefits for high school students. Firstly, sport …
  • Secondly, this increase in fitness enables students to avoid …
  • In addition, team sports help students develop useful skills, such as …
  • These skills can help them in their later lives and also in …
  • Finally, doing a team sport is a good way of …

Write your paragraph here (aim for 100–150 words): (এখানে তোমার paragraph লেখো — ১০০-১৫০টি শব্দ লেখার চেষ্টা করো)

  1. Firstly
  2. Secondly
  3. such as
  4. In addition
  5. Finally
  6. As a result
Section 9B — Error Correction
  1. …promotes the development of muscles… (wrong word order; also, comma needed: Firstly,)
  2. …enables players to avoid obesity (missing 'to' before 'avoid')
  3. …develop useful teamwork skills… (remove 's' from teamwork — uncountable; remove 'a')
  4. …sport is a good way of making new friends… (remove 'a' before 'new friends')
  5. Doing sport is popular… (remove 'a' — doing sport = uncountable gerund)
Section 10 — Guided Writing (Model Paragraph for Teacher Reference)

Doing team sports has many benefits for high school students. Firstly, sport promotes the development of the body and muscles, which helps to keep students fit and healthy. Secondly, this increase in physical fitness enables students to avoid serious health problems, such as obesity, heart disease, and high blood pressure. In addition, team sports help students develop useful group skills, such as teamwork and strategies. These skills can benefit them in their later lives and in their future jobs. Finally, doing a team sport is also a good way of making new friends and developing social skills, which are important for a successful life.

Teacher Notes

Unit 2 — Sport: Teaching Tips & Notes

  • This is the first Task 2 unit in the book. Take time to ensure students understand the difference between Task 1 and Task 2 before beginning.
  • Section 3 (Warm-Up): Do Q3 (Task 2 structure) orally as a class. Draw the 5-part essay structure on the board for visual learners.
  • Section 4 (Essay Structure Table): Remind students that IELTS markers deduct marks for essays that do not address the question. The topic sentence of each body paragraph must be clearly connected to the question.
  • Section 5 (Model Text): Ask students to underline signpost words in the model first, before answering the comprehension questions. This helps them notice the text structure.
  • Section 7 (Countable vs Uncountable): This is a very common error for Bangladeshi learners — especially 'a teamwork', 'a fitness', 'a participation'. Drill these corrections explicitly.
  • Section 7B (Articles): Be aware that some nouns (e.g. 'development') can be both countable and uncountable depending on context. Accept both correct interpretations.
  • Section 9B (Error Correction): Do the first one as a class example. Then let students work in pairs. This builds proofreading skills critical for the exam.
  • Section 10 (Guided Writing): Collect and mark using IELTS Task 2 criteria. Focus feedback on: (1) use of signpost words, (2) noun countability, (3) article accuracy.
