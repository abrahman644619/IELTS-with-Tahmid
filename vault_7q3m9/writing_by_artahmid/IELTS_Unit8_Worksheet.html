Grammar Focus 1 | Prepositions for Time | সময়ের Prepositions
Grammar Note | ব্যাকরণ নোট

Line graph বর্ণনা করার সময় সময়কাল বোঝাতে এই prepositions ব্যবহার করো:

  • between … and — দুটো সময়ের মধ্যে। → between 1980 and 1986 / between January and July
  • from … to — শুরু থেকে শেষ। → from 1977 to 2001 / from January to July
  • from … until — শুরু থেকে শেষ পর্যন্ত। → from 1990 until 2004 / from January until December
  • in — একটি নির্দিষ্ট বছর বা মাসে। → in 1989 / in February
  • for — একটি নির্দিষ্ট সময়ের ব্যাপ্তি। → for five years / for the three-month period
  • during — কোনো একটি সময়কালে। → during the 1980s / during 1994
  • over — একটি সময়কাল জুড়ে। → over five years / over the three-month period

Useful phrase: in three-yearly intervals / in two-yearly intervals

Grammar Focus 2 | Prepositions for Numbers | সংখ্যার Prepositions
Grammar Note | ব্যাকরণ নোট

from … to → Movement (পরিবর্তন দেখাতে — verb + from X to Y):

Ex: The number rose from 120 million to 126 million. / It fell from 126 to 98.

at → State (স্থির মান বা অবস্থা দেখাতে):

Ex: It peaked at 126 million. / It remained stable at 95 million. / The number began at 120 million.

of → With nouns (noun-এর পরে পরিমাণ বোঝাতে):

Ex: There was an increase of 5 million. / There was a drop of 2 million.

Language Focus | Referencing Pronouns | পুনরাবৃত্তি এড়াও
Grammar Note | ব্যাকরণ নোট

একই শব্দ বারবার লিখলে IELTS-এ marks কমে। 'it', 'they', 'them' ব্যবহার করে পুনরাবৃত্তি এড়াও।

  • it → singular subject বা object-এর জন্য।
  • they → plural subject-এর জন্য।
  • them → plural object-এর জন্য।

Example: The population increased in 1992. The population increased again in 1993.

Better: The population increased in 1992. It increased again in 1993.

Section 9C — Model Paragraph

Rapid population growth during the twentieth century caused a lot of environmental damage. It put a lot of pressure on land and other natural resources, such as energy and water. It also created problems such as pollution, changing weather patterns, and the loss of many species of plants, birds, and animals. Many species of plants, birds, and animals died because urban development and agriculture destroyed their habitat. They cannot be brought back. Towards the end of the 1990s, scientists realised that the world was getting warmer. They now believe that this was due to air pollution caused by industrial development, planes, and vehicles.

Teacher Notes

Unit 8 — Environment: Teaching Tips

  • This worksheet includes 4 HD line graphs: Graph 1 (Birds, Model 1), Graph 2 (CO₂, Model 2), Graph 3a (Sea Level, solo practice), Graph 3b (CO₂ West vs East, two-line practice).
  • Section 4A (Comprehension): Ask students to cover the model and answer from the graph alone. This forces active reading of graph features before passive reading of the model.
  • Section 5B (Adjectives → Adverbs): The most common errors are 'gradual' → 'gradually' (misspelled) and 'steady' → 'steadily' (not 'steadyly'). Drill the -y → -ily rule explicitly.
  • Section 6 (Prepositions for Time): 'over' and 'during' are frequently confused. 'Over five years' emphasises duration; 'during five years' is less common and often unnatural. Use 'over'.
  • Section 7 (Prepositions for Numbers): The 'from/to' vs 'at' distinction is critical. Drill: movement → from X to Y; state → at X. 'from/to' are NEVER used for states.
  • Section 8A (Past Simple): Emphasise the 6 key irregular verbs: fall/fell, rise/rose, grow/grew are the three most important for graph description. Make students write them 5 times each.
  • Section 9 (Referencing Pronouns): Common error — using 'it' for a plural noun ('emissions… it increased'). Rule: 'emissions' is plural → always 'they'. 'The number' is singular → always 'it'.
  • Section 10B (Gap-Fill): Give students 10 minutes to complete before revealing Model 2. Even wrong answers generate engagement.
  • Section 11B (Two-Line Graph): Walk students through the 'Tip for Two-Line Graphs' box carefully. Many students describe both lines in one muddled paragraph. Insist on separate treatment, then a comparison sentence using while, whereas, or In contrast.
  • Islamic stewardship note: The topic of environmental change (birds declining, CO₂ rising, sea levels increasing) connects directly to the Islamic concept of khilafa (stewardship of the Earth). The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said: 'If the Hour is about to come and one of you has a seedling in his hand, let him plant it.' Use this to motivate students beyond academic performance — environmental concern is an act of worship.
