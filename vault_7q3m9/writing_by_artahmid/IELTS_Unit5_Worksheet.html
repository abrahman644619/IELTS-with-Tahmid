এই worksheet শেষ করলে তুমি পারবে —

  • Read and interpret data from a two-period comparison table.
  • Use key vocabulary for describing changes: increase, decrease, drop, rise, remain, stay unchanged, fourfold, considerably, significantly.
  • Use word suffixes correctly: -ion, -ance, -ment, -ive, -able, -ing, -ed.
  • Use 'overall', 'in general', 'in particular' to introduce the main trend.
  • Use past simple tense accurately for describing past data.
  • Vary noun phrases using 5 different styles.
  • Use 'there was/were + adjective + noun' as an alternative sentence structure.
  • Write a structured Task 1 paragraph describing a table.

Model 1 — Table 1 Description

The table illustrates the number of students from four different countries who attended tertiary institutions in a western country in 2000 and 2005. Overall, the number of international students dropped slightly over this five-year period and, in particular, there was a significant decrease in student numbers from China, although it still remained the largest group. There was a fourfold rise in Brazilian student enrolments.

In 2000, the number of students who came from China, at 35,000, was more than twice the number of Indian students, at 15,000. Smaller numbers came from Japan and Brazil, at 6,000 and 2,000 respectively. In 2005, the proportion of students changed considerably. Chinese student numbers decreased to 22,000, but there was a dramatic increase in the number of Brazilian students, which went from 2,000 to 8,000. Indian student numbers stayed mostly unchanged, at around 16,000, and there was a small decrease in the number of Japanese students, from 6,000 in 2000 to 4,000 in 2005.

Grammar Focus 1 | Verb Tenses for Tables | Tense সঠিকভাবে ব্যবহার করো
Grammar Note | ব্যাকরণ নোট

KEY RULE: যদি table-এ PAST time frame থাকে (e.g. 2000 and 2005), সব verb PAST SIMPLE tense-এ লিখতে হবে।

Exception: প্রথম sentence (topic sentence) সাধারণত PRESENT SIMPLE — কারণ table এখনও exist করছে।

Example: The table illustrates [PRESENT] … students who attended [PAST] … . Numbers dropped [PAST] from 35,000 to 22,000.

Important verbs to know:

was / were  |  dropped  |  increased  |  rose  |  fell  |  decreased  |  remained  |  stayed  |  came  |  went  |  changed

Language Focus 1 | 'overall', 'in general', 'in particular'
Grammar Note | ব্যাকরণ নোট
  • overall → সামগ্রিক প্রবণতা বোঝাতে। প্রায়ই sentence-এর শুরুতে ব্যবহার হয়।
  • in general → সাধারণ trend বলতে। 'overall'-এর মতো।

Example: Overall, the number of students dropped slightly and, in particular, Chinese numbers fell dramatically.

Language Focus 3 | 'there was/were' Structure
Grammar Note | ব্যাকরণ নোট

Table বর্ণনায় sentence structure varied করার জন্য 'there was/were' ব্যবহার করো।

Style 1 — verb + adverb: The number of students from Somalia increased significantly.

Style 2 — there was + adj + noun: There was a significant increase in the number of students from Somalia.

Pattern: There was a [adjective] [noun] in/of + [subject].

Teacher Notes

Unit 5 — Education: Study Abroad: Teaching Tips

  • This worksheet includes 3 professionally formatted tables AND 3 grouped bar charts — one for each dataset. The charts provide a visual representation of the table trends to aid comprehension.
  • Section 3 (Warm-Up): The study abroad discussion naturally connects to Islamic values of seeking knowledge ('Seek knowledge, even unto China') — a brief discussion can motivate engagement with the topic.
  • Section 4 (Table 1): Before students read Model 1, ask them to look at the bar chart and describe what they see in Bengali. Then ask them to read the table. This two-step visual approach activates schema.
  • Section 6B (Change Vocabulary): Drill increase/rise/grow and decrease/fall/drop as sets. Also drill adjective + noun pairs: a dramatic increase, a significant fall, a slight rise, a considerable decrease.
  • Section 7A (Tense): This is a common error — students write 'increases' (present) when they should write 'increased' (past). Emphasise: 'check the years first, then choose your tense.'
  • Section 8 (Overall/In particular): Model this on the board: write an incomplete overall sentence and ask students to complete it using table data. Practice the pattern: 'Overall, ___ and, in particular, ___.'
  • Section 9B (Noun Phrase Sentences): Insist students use varied styles. Penalise repeating the same structure. Pair work — each student uses different styles and compares with a partner.
  • Section 10 (There was/were): Model both styles side by side on the board and ask students to identify which is more 'academic sounding'. Both are correct; the goal is variety.
