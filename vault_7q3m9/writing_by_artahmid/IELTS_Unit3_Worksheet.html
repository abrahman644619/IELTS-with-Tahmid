শেখার লক্ষ্য | Learning Objectives

এই worksheet শেষ করলে তুমি পারবে —

  • Read and interpret data from a bar chart.
  • Use 'in relation to' to explain what a graph compares.
  • Use approximate language: approximately, about, around.
  • Identify words functioning as verb, noun, or adjective (average, decrease, score, view, show).
  • Use correct modifiers: many / fewer (countable) vs much / less (uncountable) vs a lot of (both).
  • Use the relative pronoun 'who' to describe groups of people.
  • Write a structured bar chart paragraph: topic sentence + overall trend + data details.

Model 1

The bar graph shows the average science test scores of students in Grade 8 in relation to the hours they spend watching television. Overall, the students who watch less television have higher scores, and the average test score decreases as the number of viewing hours increases. The highest score of 80 is for students who usually watch one hour or less of television a day. The next highest score is approximately 79. This is for students who average two hours of television viewing. Students who watch no television and students who watch about 3 hours of television per day have a score of around 76. The lowest score of approximately 65 is for students who view six or more hours of television daily.

Grammar Focus | ব্যাকরণ
7A — Words as Verbs, Nouns, or Adjectives
Grammar Note | ব্যাকরণ নোট

ইংরেজিতে অনেক শব্দ একই রূপে verb, noun বা adjective হিসেবে ব্যবহার হতে পারে।

decrease as VERB: The score decreases each year. (action — verb)

decrease as NOUN: There is a decrease in scores. (subject/object — noun)

average as VERB: Students average 4 hours per day. (what they do)

average as ADJ: The average score is 76. (describes 'score')

Decide if the bracketed word is a Verb (V), Noun (N), or Adjective (Adj).

7B — Modifiers: many/fewer vs much/less vs a lot of
Grammar Note | ব্যাকরণ নোট

many / fewer → Countable noun-এর সাথে। Ex: many hours / fewer students

much / less → Uncountable noun-এর সাথে। Ex: much time / less television

a lot of → Countable ও uncountable উভয়ের সাথে। Ex: a lot of hours / a lot of time

Teacher Notes

Unit 3 — Media: Teaching Tips & Notes

  • This worksheet includes 3 bar charts embedded directly — no separate printout needed.
  • Section 4 (Chart Reading): Ask students to cover the model and describe Chart 1 in their own words first — this activates prior language.
  • Section 5 (Model Text): Ask students to underline (1) 'in relation to', (2) 'who', and (3) approximate words before answering the questions.
  • Section 6B (Similar or Different): 'fewer' and 'greater' are opposites. 'number' and 'figure' are similar. These are key IELTS vocabulary pairs.
  • Section 7A (Word Functions): This is the most challenging grammar point in the unit. Spend extra time with board examples. Bangladeshi learners often struggle because Bengali does not inflect this way.
  • Section 7B (Modifiers): The key error is 'less students' — drill: 'fewer students' (countable) / 'less time' (uncountable). 'A lot of' works for both — encourage students to use it when unsure.
  • Section 7C (who): Emphasise that 'who' is ONLY for people. 'Which' is for objects/things.
  • Section 9 (Guided Writing): The sentence starters reduce cognitive load. Encourage students to vary the structure once comfortable.
