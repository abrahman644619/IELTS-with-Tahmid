Model 1 — Hosting the Olympic Games: Benefits and Drawbacks

Hosting the Olympic Games has become a huge undertaking because of the cost of preparation and the overall financial risks involved. However, it is a wonderful opportunity for the host country because of the chance for worldwide publicity and the prestige associated with the Games. This essay will discuss some of the problems and also the positive aspects of being a host for the Olympics.

There can be no doubt that holding the Olympics involves financial risks. It is possible to lose money because of the expense of the special buildings and facilities needed for the Games. For example, the original budget of under €5 billion for the Athens Olympics grew to €11 billion, and that was a shock for the Greek government. In addition, most countries have a limited budget for building projects, and if it is all spent on preparations for the Games, this may mean there is less for other essential projects.

On the other hand, there are major benefits to be gained by hosting the Olympics. It is certainly a wonderful showcase for the host country because of the global interest and TV coverage it generates. It also ensures significant investment in facilities such as stadiums, roads, water supply, and accommodation. An additional bonus is the possible profit generated by the sale of TV rights, sponsorship, ticket sales, and visitor expenditure. Over the 20 years to 2000, every country hosting the Olympics either broke even or made a profit.

Essential Grammar | 'because of' + Noun Phrase
Grammar Note | ব্যাকরণ নোট

কারণ বোঝাতে আমরা দুটি উপায় ব্যবহার করি:

because + clause (subject + verb) → Prices went up because the demand was high.

because of + noun phrase → Prices went up because of high demand.

Example from Model 1: Hosting the Olympic Games has become a huge undertaking because of the cost of preparation and the overall financial risks involved.

6A — Combine Sentences Using 'because of' | বাক্য যুক্ত করো

Combine each pair of sentences using 'because of' + noun phrase. You will need to rewrite the sentence in brackets as a noun phrase. The example shows you how. (প্রতিটি জোড়া বাক্যকে 'because of' + noun phrase ব্যবহার করে একটিতে মেলাও।)

Example | উদাহরণ

It is certainly a wonderful showcase for the host country. (It generates global interest and TV coverage.)

→ It is certainly a wonderful showcase for the host country because of the global interest and TV coverage it generates.

a. Athletes all over the world want to compete in the Olympics. (There is the chance to win a gold medal and become famous.)
b. The Japanese authorities spent half of their budget on security. (They had fears of a terrorist attack.)
c. A lot of money had to be spent on media facilities and fibre-optic cables. (The location of the G8 summit was remote.)
d. Millions of people all over the world learn more about the Olympic host country. (The resulting TV coverage ensures this.)
e. The Olympic host country can make a profit. (The sale of TV rights, sponsorship, and ticket sales make this possible.)
f. There may be some drawbacks to hosting the Games. (The cost of buildings and preparations is high.)
g. However, the advantages outweigh the drawbacks. (The host country gains a lot of status and publicity as a result of the Games.)
h. International events represent a huge undertaking for all those involved. (There is a huge financial outlay and organisation involved in the preparations for these events.)

Language Focus | 'this', 'these', 'that' | Demonstrative Determiners
Grammar Note | ব্যাকরণ নোট

Written English-এ আমরা প্রায়ই 'this', 'these', এবং 'that' ব্যবহার করি আগের idea-কে refer করতে। এটি cohesion (সংযুক্ততা) তৈরি করে।

'this' → singular idea বা uncountable noun-এর জন্য।

'these' → plural noun বা multiple ideas-এর জন্য।

'that' → আগের একটি নির্দিষ্ট বিষয় বা ঘটনার জন্য।

উদাহরণ (Model 1 থেকে): ...if it is all spent on preparations for the Games, this may mean there is less for other essential projects.
[this = a lot of money being spent on preparation for the Games]

উদাহরণ: ...the budget grew to €11 billion, and that was a shock for the Greek government.
[that = the fact that the budget more than doubled in size]

Signpost Words | Essay-এর পথ দেখানো শব্দ

Signpost Words | কোন words কখন ব্যবহার করবে — Essay-এ signpost words পাঠককে বুঝতে সাহায্য করে পরবর্তী idea কী ধরনের হবে।

সমর্থন করতে / আরও তথ্য যোগ করতে (Supporting an idea): There can be no doubt that... • For example, ... • In addition, ... • Also, ... • An additional bonus is... • It is certainly...

বিপরীত (contrasting) idea introduce করতে: However, ... • On the other hand, ...

Note: 'On the other hand' signals an OPPOSITE idea — NOT just additional information. It has the same meaning as 'However'.

Model 2 — Hosting the G8 Summit: Benefits and Drawbacks

Hosting the G8 summit is an important opportunity for any country because of the publicity and the possible political benefits. However, it has become a huge undertaking because of the soaring cost of preparation and the overall security risks it involves. In this essay, I will discuss some of the benefits to be gained by being a host for the G8 summit and also consider some of the disadvantages.

There can be no doubt that there are major benefits to be gained by hosting the G8 summit. The cost of organising the summit itself increased tenfold. In addition, half of the budget was spent on a massive security operation due to the fear of a terrorist attack.

Teacher Notes

Unit 18 — International Events: Teaching Tips & Notes

  • This unit builds directly on the Task 2 essay-structure skills from earlier units. Confirm students can already identify a thesis statement and body paragraph topic sentences before starting.
  • Section 4 (Model 1): This is a two-sided (advantages/disadvantages) essay, not a one-sided opinion essay. Emphasise that the thesis statement in this essay type previews BOTH sides, unlike a straightforward opinion essay.
  • Section 6 ('because of'): A very common Bangladeshi-learner error is writing "because of" followed by a full clause (e.g. "because of the demand was high"). Drill the noun-phrase-only rule explicitly with several quick oral examples.
  • Section 7 (Demonstrative determiners): Students often overuse 'that' when 'this'/'these' is more natural, or fail to make it agree in number with what it refers to. The 7C exercise is useful diagnostic — go through it together first if the class is weaker.
  • Section 8 (Signpost words): Stress that 'On the other hand' must signal a genuine contrast, not just another supporting point — a frequent misuse in student essays that costs Coherence and Cohesion marks.
  • Section 10 (G8 Summit — Ordering Task): This is a strong classroom activity for pairs — have students justify their ordering choices aloud, referring to signpost words and pronoun reference as clues.
  • International cooperation and competitive events, when conducted fairly and without excess or wasteful spending, reflect values Islam encourages — mutual respect between nations, honest effort, and responsible stewardship of resources. This can be woven naturally into class discussion of the Olympic and G8 budget figures.
