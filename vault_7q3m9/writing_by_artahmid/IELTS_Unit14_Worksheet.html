এই worksheet শেষ করলে তুমি পারবে —

  • Read and interpret a line graph showing changes over time, including future predictions.
  • Use 15 key vocabulary words for describing price and economic changes.
  • Describe changes in TWO ways: verb + adverb (rose sharply) AND adjective + noun (a sharp rise).
  • Use future prediction language: 'will', 'it is predicted/forecast that...'
  • Use prepositions of time correctly: over, for, before, after, during, in, at.
  • Write a full Task 1 paragraph about a line graph that includes both past data and future predictions.

Model 1 — Oil Prices: 1995 with Forecasts to 2015

The line graph shows oil prices in dollars per barrel from 1995 with forecasts to 2015. Overall, oil prices increased by nearly five hundred per cent from 1995 to 2009, with the most dramatic increase in the three years from 2006 to 2009.

This rise is predicted to continue into the future. Prices started at twenty dollars a barrel during 1995 and then rose slightly to approximately twenty-four dollars in 1998.

The cost of a barrel then dropped for two years, to the lowest point of about sixteen dollars during 2000.

After that, there was a steady price rise, reaching about fifty dollars a barrel in 2006.

This was followed by a dramatic climb to one hundred dollars in 2009, and it is forecast that this rise will continue into the future and will level off slightly after 2010.

Essential Grammar | Describing Changes Two Ways | পরিবর্তন বর্ণনার দুটি উপায়
Grammar Note | ব্যাকরণ নোট

Line graph বর্ণনায় পরিবর্তন দুটি উপায়ে বলা যায়। দুটির অর্থ একই — শুধু sentence structure ভিন্ন।

উপায় ১ — Verb + Adverb: Subject + verb + adverb
Ex: In 1991, the price increased suddenly. (দাম হঠাৎ বেড়ে গেল।)

উপায় ২ — Adjective + Noun: There was a + adjective + noun + (in + subject)
Ex: In 1991, there was a sudden increase in price. (দামে হঠাৎ বৃদ্ধি হলো।)

মূল কথা: adverb (suddenly) → adjective (sudden)  |  verb (increase) → noun (increase)  |  IELTS-এ উভয় উপায় ব্যবহার করলে sentence variety আসে।

6A — Convert Between Two Structures | দুটি Structure-এ বদলাও

Change each sentence to use the OTHER structure. Watch word order carefully. The first one is done for you. (প্রতিটি বাক্যকে অন্য structure-এ লেখো।)

Example | উদাহরণ

Verb + Adverb: Oil prices rose dramatically from 2006 to 2009.
Adjective + Noun: There was a dramatic rise in oil prices from 2006 to 2009.

a. In 2004, the cost of oil increased slightly to $26 per barrel. (Verb+Adv)
b. There was a moderate climb in house prices in 2007. (Adj+Noun)
c. There will be a significant fall in oil prices over the next two years. (Adj+Noun)
d. Enrolments at university dropped slightly last year. (Verb+Adv)
e. There was a dramatic increase in house prices over a twenty-year period. (Adj+Noun)
f. Beef prices rose steadily for two years to reach a peak of $22 per kilo. (Verb+Adv)
g. The cost of a house dropped suddenly in 1992. (Verb+Adv)
h. There was a dramatic rise in gold prices last month, from $700 to $900 per ounce. (Adj+Noun)

Essential Grammar | Future Predictions | ভবিষ্যৎ পূর্বাভাস
Grammar Note | ব্যাকরণ নোট

Line graph-এ যদি future data থাকে (predicted/forecast), তখন language পরিবর্তন হয়। Task 1-এ আমরা 'might', 'could', 'may' ব্যবহার করি না — শুধু 'will' ব্যবহার করি।

Structure 1 — will: Prices will rise over the next ten years.

Structure 2 — It is predicted/forecast that: It is predicted that prices will continue to rise. It is forecast that the price will level off after 2010.

Structure 3 — noun (for introductions): The graph includes a forecast/prediction for the next twenty years.

Language Focus | Prepositions of Time | সময়ের Prepositions
Grammar Note | ব্যাকরণ নোট

সময়ের পরিবর্তন স্পষ্টভাবে বোঝাতে সঠিক prepositions ব্যবহার করতে হয়।

  • over একটি সময়কাল জুড়ে। → over five years / over the next decade
  • for একটি নির্দিষ্ট সময়ের ব্যাপ্তি। → for two years / for the three-month period
  • before কোনো সময় বা ঘটনার আগে। → before dropping again / before 1990
  • after কোনো সময় বা ঘটনার পরে। → after 2010 / after the sudden drop
  • during কোনো একটি সময়কালে। → during 1995 / during the 1980s
  • in একটি নির্দিষ্ট বছর বা মাসে। → in 1989 / in February
  • at একটি নির্দিষ্ট মূল্য বা মাত্রায়। → at $50 a barrel / at the peak
