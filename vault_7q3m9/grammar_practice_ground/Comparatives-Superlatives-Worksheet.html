Simple Explanation (বাংলা সহ)

Read this before starting the activities

আমরা comparative ব্যবহার করি দুটি জিনিস তুলনা করতে: Dhaka is bigger than Khulna.

আমরা superlative ব্যবহার করি তিন বা তার বেশি জিনিসের মধ্যে তুলনা করতে: Dhaka is the largest city in Bangladesh.

1. Short Adjectives (+er / +est)
tall → taller → tallest
fast → faster → fastest
small → smaller → smallest
Example: This road is wider than the old one.
2. Long Adjectives (more / most)
beautiful → more beautiful → most beautiful
expensive → more expensive → most expensive
Example: This hotel is more expensive than that one.
3. Irregular Forms
good → better → best
bad → worse → worst
far → farther/further → farthest/furthest

Examples:

  1. This building is taller than that one.
  2. She is the best student in the class.
  3. This phone is more expensive than the old model.
  4. The Nile is one of the longest rivers in the world.
  5. Today is hotter than yesterday.
