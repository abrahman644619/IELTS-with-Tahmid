Common prepositions: in, on, at, to, for, with, by, from, about

1. Time
in → months, years, long periods (in 2025, in July)
on → specific days (on Monday, on my birthday)
at → exact time (at 7 pm, at midnight)
2. Place
in → inside something (in the room)
on → surface (on the table)
at → specific point (at the station)
3. Direction
go to school  |  travel from Dhaka  |  walk into the room
4. Common IELTS Collocations
interested in  |  responsible for  |  afraid of  |  good at  |  depend on

Examples:

  1. She lives in Dhaka.
  2. The meeting is at 10 am.
  3. I placed the book on the table.
  4. He travelled from Dhaka to Chittagong.
  5. She is interested in learning English.
