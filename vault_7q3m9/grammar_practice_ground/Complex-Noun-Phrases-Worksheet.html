Students will learn how to recognise and build complex noun phrases (pre-modifiers + head noun + post-modifiers) — a key technique for high-band IELTS Writing and Speaking.

Student Information

Simple Explanation (বাংলা সহ)

Read this before starting the activities

Noun phrase অর্থ: head noun-এর সাথে modifier যুক্ত হয়ে তৈরি অংশ, যা বাক্যে subject/object হিসেবে কাজ করে। Complex noun phrase-এ head noun-এর সাথে একাধিক pre-modifier (আগে) ও post-modifier (পরে) যুক্ত থাকে, যা তথ্যকে ঘনীভূত (condensed) করে তোলে — এটি IELTS-এর high-band writing/speaking-এর জন্য গুরুত্বপূর্ণ কৌশল।

Pre-modifier (আগে বসে) → article/determiner + adjective(s) + noun
Example: a rapidly growing economy
Post-modifier: prepositional phrase
Example: the rise in youth unemployment
Post-modifier: relative clause
Example: the policy that was introduced last year
Post-modifier: participle phrase / to-infinitive phrase
Example: the report published yesterday / the decision to expand the factory

Examples:

  1. The rapid increase in housing prices has worried many young families. (pre-modifier "rapid" + post-modifier prepositional phrase "in housing prices")
  2. The report published by the committee revealed shocking data. (post-modifier: participle phrase, passive meaning)
  3. Students who fail to submit their assignments on time will lose marks. (post-modifier: relative clause)
