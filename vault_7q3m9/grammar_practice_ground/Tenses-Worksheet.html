Simple Explanation (বাংলা সহ)

Read this before starting the activities

Tense বলে কোন কাজ (action) কখন ঘটেছে তা বোঝায় — present (বর্তমান), past (অতীত), future (ভবিষ্যৎ)। প্রতিটি টাইম-ফ্রেমে continuous (চলমান), perfect (সম্পন্ন), এবং perfect continuous (দীর্ঘকাল ধরে সম্পন্ন) রূপ থাকতে পারে।

Present Simple: habitual / facts → I study English.
Present Continuous: now / temporary → I am studying now.
Present Perfect: past action with present result / experience → I have studied English.
Past Simple: finished past action → I studied yesterday.
Past Continuous: ongoing past action → I was studying at 8 pm.
Past Perfect: action before another past action → I had studied before the test.
Future (will / going to): prediction / plan → I will study. / I am going to study.

Quick examples:

  1. I work in a college. (Present Simple)
  2. She is working on a project now. (Present Continuous)
  3. They have finished the report. (Present Perfect)
  4. He went to Dhaka last week. (Past Simple)
  5. While I was walking, it started to rain. (Past Continuous + Past Simple)
  6. By the time we arrived, the film had started. (Past Perfect)
  7. I will call you tomorrow. (Future — will)
  8. She is going to visit Chittagong next month. (Future — going to)
