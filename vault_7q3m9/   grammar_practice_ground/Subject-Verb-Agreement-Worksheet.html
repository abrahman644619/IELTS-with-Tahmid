Simple Explanation (বাংলা সহ)

Read this before starting the activities

Subject–verb agreement অর্থ: বাক্যের কর্তা (subject) এবং ক্রিয়া (verb) সংখ্যায় (singular/plural) মিলবে।

  • একবচন subject → একবচন verb (e.g., She works.)
  • বহুবচন subject → বহুবচন verb (e.g., They work.)
1. Collective Nouns
team, family, staff, committee — সাধারণত একবচন verb নেয় (একটি একক হিসেবে চিন্তা করলে)।
Example: The team is winning the match.
2. Intervening Phrases
along with, as well as, together with — এই phrase গুলো verb-কে প্রভাবিত করে না; মূল subject-এর সাথেই verb মিলবে।
Example: The manager, along with his assistants, is coming.
3. Quantifiers & Special Cases
a number of → plural verb  |  the number of → singular verb
each / every / neither / none → সাধারণত singular verb
Either A or B / Neither A nor B → verb মিলবে কাছের (nearest) subject-এর সাথে
Example: A number of students have passed; the number of students is increasing.
Example: Neither Rafiq nor his friends were present.

Examples:

  1. The list of books is on the table. (list = subject → singular)
  2. My family is going to visit Cox's Bazar. (family = collective → usually singular)
  3. Either the manager or the assistants have the keys. (nearest subject = assistants → plural)
