Student Information

Simple Explanation (বাংলা সহ)

Read this before starting the activities

Cohesive device দুটি ধারণা বা বাক্যকে যুক্তিসঙ্গতভাবে জোড়া দেয়। Cause & effect device কারণ-ফলাফল সম্পর্ক বোঝায়, আর contrast device দুটি ধারণার মধ্যে বৈপরীত্য/পার্থক্য বোঝায়। ব্যাকরণগতভাবে এগুলো তিন ধরনের হয়: preposition (+ noun/gerund), conjunction (+ clause), এবং connector/adverb (+ independent clause, semicolon/period-এর পরে বসে)।

Cause, preposition + noun → due to, because of, owing to
Example: due to heavy rain
Cause, conjunction + clause → because, since, as
Example: because it rained heavily
Effect, connector → therefore, as a result, consequently, thus
Example: It rained heavily; therefore, the match was cancelled.
Contrast, preposition + noun → despite, in spite of  |  Contrast, conjunction + clause → although, even though, whereas
Example: despite the rain / although it rained

Examples:

  1. Due to heavy traffic, she arrived late. (preposition + noun)
  2. Because the traffic was heavy, she arrived late. (conjunction + clause)
  3. The traffic was heavy; as a result, she arrived late. (connector)
  4. Despite the heavy traffic, she arrived on time. (contrast, preposition)
  5. Although the traffic was heavy, she arrived on time. (contrast, conjunction)
