Student Information

Simple Explanation (বাংলা সহ)

Read this before starting the activities

Nominalization অর্থ: verb বা adjective-কে noun-এ রূপান্তর করা, যা লেখাকে বেশি formal, compact ও academic করে তোলে। সাধারণত নির্দিষ্ট suffix যোগ করে এটি করা হয় (-tion, -sion, -ment, -ance, -ence, -ity, -ness, -y)।

Verb → Noun
decide → decision, develop → development, analyze → analysis, improve → improvement, discover → discovery
Adjective → Noun
happy → happiness, difficult → difficulty, popular → popularity, responsible → responsibility
Nominalization ব্যবহার করলে verb-heavy বাক্য noun-heavy হয়ে যায়, এবং প্রায়ই আরও impersonal/academic tone তৈরি হয় — IELTS Writing Task 2-এর জন্য উপযোগী।

Examples:

  1. সাধারণ: They decided to increase the budget, so costs went down.
  2. Formal (nominalized): The decision to increase the budget resulted in a reduction in costs.
  3. সাধারণ: The government reduced taxes, which made people happier.
  4. Formal (nominalized): The government's reduction in taxes led to an increase in public happiness.
