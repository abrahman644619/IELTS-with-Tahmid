Listening Module
Tips · Free Tests · Vocabulary
🎧
Tip 01
Read Questions Before Listening
Always skim the questions before the audio begins. This helps you anticipate what information to listen for and avoid being caught off guard.
Strategy
Tip 02
Listen for Signpost Language
Phrases like "however," "the main point is," and "to summarise" signal critical information. Training your ear for these cues boosts accuracy.
Comprehension
Tip 03
Don't Panic if You Miss Something
If you miss an answer, move on immediately. Lingering on a missed answer causes you to miss the next one too. You only hear the audio once.
Test Strategy
Tip 04
Spelling Counts — Every Letter!
In IELTS Listening, spelling errors mean a wrong answer. Practise spelling carefully. Pay attention to plural forms and verb tenses.
Accuracy
Tip 05
Understand All Four Sections
Section 1: social conversation. Section 2: monologue. Section 3: academic discussion. Section 4: academic lecture. Difficulty increases progressively.
Test Format
Tip 06
Watch Out for Distractors
Speakers often correct themselves — "Actually, let me change that to...". The final answer given is correct. Train yourself to catch these changes.
Trap Avoidance
Tip 07
Practise with Native-Speed Audio
IELTS uses native-speed British, Australian, and American English. Listen to BBC Radio, TED Talks, and podcasts daily to train your ear.
Preparation
Tip 08
Use the 10-Minute Transfer Time
At the end of the Listening test you get 10 minutes to transfer answers. Use this to check spelling, grammar, and that you haven't exceeded any word limits.
Time Management
Tip 09
Predict the Answer Type
Before listening, determine what type of answer is expected — a number, a name, a place, an adjective. This narrows your focus when the audio plays.
Strategy
Tip 10
Use Capital Letters for Proper Nouns
Names of people, places, and organisations should be capitalised. Get into the habit of writing them correctly from the start to avoid losing marks.
Accuracy
